The **Scout's Honor** is an [mystical item](Equipment.md#Mystical_Items) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that is obtained by transforming the [![](../../images/Strange_Gem.png)](Strange%20Gem.md) [Strange Gem](Strange%20Gem.md) after bestowing the four [amulets](Amulets.md) upon the [Stone Scout](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stone_Scout) found at the [Peak](Peak%20%28biome%29.md). Scouts can hold ![Mouse left.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Mouse_left.png/25px-Mouse_left.png?60ed49) **Left Mouse** to commune with it, distorting the screen heavily before transporting all players to [Nadir](Nadir.md).

When [cooked](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cooked), the Scout's Honor explodes into a **40 meter** Anti-Sphere that launches all scouts within away from the center before quickly vanishing. Additionally, the [Scoutmaster](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scoutmaster) will be spawned targeting the player who cooked it, chasing them for up to **1 hour**.
