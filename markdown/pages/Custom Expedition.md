# Custom Expedition

[![](../../images/Custom_expedition_ui.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ACustom_expedition_ui.png)

Custom Expedition settings.

**Custom Expeditions** were added in version [1.60.a](1.60.a.md) ("The Play It Your Way Update"). They provide numerous options to adjust [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) spawns, enabled [hazards](Hazards.md), and other gameplay elements.

Earning [Badges](Badges.md) and [Sashes](Ascents.md) is disabled in Custom Expeditions.

## Details

In a Custom Expedition, most [items](Items.md) can be removed from the spawn pool. Since items spawn is based on [weights](Rarity.md), other items become more common, with the [![](../../images/Dynamite.png)](Dynamite.md) [Dynamite](Dynamite.md) being especially notable. If a [Luggage](Luggage.md) or [Ancient Statue](Ancient%20Statue.md) is opened with no available items, [![](../../images/Bing_Bong.png)](Bing%20Bong.md) [Bing Bong](Bing%20Bong.md) will be spawned as a fallback.

| **Fall Damage** | - Off - Low - Normal - High | Depending on the chosen setting, can halve, double, or disable [fall damage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Fall_damage). High is identical to the change in [Ascent 1](Ascents.md). |
