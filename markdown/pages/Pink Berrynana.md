The **Pink Berrynana** is a natural [food](Food.md) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found growing on [vines](Vines.md) in the [Tropics](Tropics.md). Eating a Pink Berrynana will restore [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **15** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) and will slowly inflict [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **15** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) over 6 seconds beginning 2 seconds after eating. After eating the Berrynana, the player receives a [![](../../images/Pink_Berrynana_Peel.png)](Pink%20Berrynana%20Peel.md) [Pink Berrynana Peel](Pink%20Berrynana%20Peel.md). If the Pink Berrynana is fed to another, the other player will receive the peel. If the other player's inventory and hands are full, the peel will drop at their feet.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Pink Berrynana removes the [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) effect.
