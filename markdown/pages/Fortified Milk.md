The **Fortified Milk** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. When consumed, it removes [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **50** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) and [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **10** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger), and applies [![Invincibility](../../images/Status_Invincibility.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) [Invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) for 15 seconds. For the duration, a scout cannot gain any additional [status effects](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina_Bar) other than [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight), [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) and [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify), although existing status effects are not removed. A scout can still die by some methods, like being [consumed by another scout](Cannibalism.md).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) Fortified Milk increases its duration by 10 seconds the first time you cook it. Overcooking has no effect.

If a [skeleton](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md) consumes Fortified Milk, it additionally heals [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **100** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury).
