The **Scroll** is an item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It can be found at the [campfires](Campfire.md) located at the top of each biome, excluding the [Caldera](Caldera.md) and [Gloom](Gloom.md).

The scroll can be opened to reveal a **Torn Page**. The exact page revealed depends on the player who opens the scroll. When the scroll is opened, the highest-numbered unread page that player meets the requirements for is revealed. If that player has read all the currently available pages, then an available page is spawned at random. Biome requirements are based on the closest campfire, regardless of whether it's been lit. Revealing or reading page 12 unlocks the [![Bookworm Badge](../../images/Bookworm_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bookworm_Badge) [Bookworm Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bookworm_Badge).

Each torn page is a diary entry from [Scoutmaster Myres](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md), detailing his experience on the island.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a scroll will cause it to give [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **10** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) upon use.

## Notes

- Only page 12 needs to be read to unlock the [![Bookworm Badge](../../images/Bookworm_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bookworm_Badge) [Bookworm Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bookworm_Badge). Therefore it's possible to receive the badge by only reading page 12, if another player that meets its requirements opens the scroll and gets it.
- It is impossible to see page 11 between reading pages 10 and 12, despite them seeming intended to be read in order.

## Trivia

- The Bonus rules might not have been written in numerical order.- Bonus rule 8 implies it was written after 10, despite numerically coming before it.
  - Bonus rule 10 describes the [Alpine](Alpine.md), which appears on the island before the [Gloom](Gloom.md) and [Caldera](Caldera.md), which are featured in bonus rules 8 and 9.
