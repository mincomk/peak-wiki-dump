The **Faerie Lantern** is a [mystical](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical) [equipment](Equipment.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. While lit, all players within **7.2 meters** of the lantern recover [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **2.5** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat), [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **5** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **2.5** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **5** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy), and [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **2.5** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) each second, and are immune to the Fog. The Faerie Lantern can be lit for up to **30 seconds** before running out of fuel.

The Lantern will stay lit when dropped or when placed into a dropped backpack. It is automatically extinguished if it is put away, if the containing backpack is picked up, or if it is taken out of a containing backpack.

The Faerie Lantern can be used to burn [The Ghost](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Ghost) in the [Gloom](Gloom.md), granting the player the [![Exorcist Badge](../../images/Exorcist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge) [Exorcist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Faerie Lantern while it has remaining fuel causes it to explode, restoring up to [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **25** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **25** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **25** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold), [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **25** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat), [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **25** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy), [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) **25** [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) to scouts within **4.8 meters**, depending on their distance.
