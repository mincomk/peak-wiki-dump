The **Small Egg** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in the [Gloom](Gloom.md) inside Raven nests.
A Small Egg can be cracked open to receive an [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) similar to the [![](../../images/Big_Egg.png)](Big%20Egg.md) [Big Egg](Big%20Egg.md). Has a 99% chance to spawn [Egg](Egg.md) when cracked and 1% chance to spawn a [![](../../images/Cooked_Bird.png)](Cooked%20Bird.md) [Cooked Bird](Cooked%20Bird.md).
Cooking an [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) is required for the [![Gourmand Badge](../../images/Gourmand_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Gourmand_Badge) [Gourmand Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Gourmand_Badge), as the [![](../../images/Cooked_Bird.png)](Cooked%20Bird.md) [Cooked Bird](Cooked%20Bird.md) will not count..

## Trivia

- Cooking the Egg causes the resulting [Egg](Egg.md) to also be cooked when cracked open.
