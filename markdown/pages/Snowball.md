# Snowball

Snowball
[![Snowball.png](../../images/Snowball.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ASnowball.png)
[Item type](Items.md)- [Misc](Misc.md)
Biome- [Alpine](Alpine.md)
Weight
[![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) **2.5**

The **Snowball** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that can gained by picking up Snow Piles on the ground. Snowballs can be thrown and will be destroyed if they impact a player or the environment at a fast enough velocity. Throwing a Snowball at a player will cause the player to gain [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **5 to 10** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) and will cause the player to enter a [ragdoll](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragdoll) state. Cooking a Snowball will inflict [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) to the player but will not knock them down.

Rolling a Snowball on snowy ground causes it to get larger but has no effect on its [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight). Larger snowballs apply more knockback when thrown at a player. Once a Snowball is sufficiently large, it can no longer be picked up.

## Interactions

- Thrown Snowballs can be used to knock down [Zombies](Zombie.md).
- Thrown Snowballs can be used to knock down various fruits and [![](../../images/Beehive.png)](Beehive.md) [Beehives](Beehive.md).
- Thrown Snowballs will not trigger Explosive Bombshrooms or Poison Bombshrooms but Snowballs that are rolled into or dropped onto either kind of Bombshroom will trigger them.
- A [![](../../images/Scout_Cannon.png)](Scout%20Cannon.md) [Scout Cannon](Scout%20Cannon.md) can launch a Snowball provided that it can fit in the barrel of the cannon.
