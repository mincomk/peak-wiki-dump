The **Green Crispberry** is a [natural food](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Natural_food) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found growing on bushes in the [Shore](Shore.md) and [Tropics](Tropics.md). It sometimes also appears on the bushes around the [Campfire](Campfire.md) at the top of the [Roots](Roots.md) and [Alpine](Alpine.md), around the [Hot Spring](Alpine.md#Hot_Spring) in the Alpine and [Oasis](Mesa.md#Oasis) in the [Mesa](Mesa.md), and at the foot of the [Peak](Peak%20%28biome%29.md). Eating it restores [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **5** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) and inflicts [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **10** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) over the course of 4 seconds, beginning 2 seconds after eating it.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Green Crispberry removes the [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) effect.
