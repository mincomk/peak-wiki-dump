The **Big Ghost** is a hostile [NPC](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/NPC) that may spawn in the [Gloom](Gloom.md).

## Spawning and Behavior

The Big Ghost will spawn when the [Scouts](Scout.md) stay in the [Gloom](Gloom.md)'s fog for a total of **1-3 minutes**. It will follow the nearest Scout, taking a direct path through any terrain in its way. Upon getting within **1.6 meters** of a Scout, it will stop moving and detonate after **2.1 seconds**. This timer resets if all Scouts are more than **2.08 meters** away. The explosion has a radius of **16 meters** and applies [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **35** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold), [Blindness](Flash%20Bulbs.md) for 20 seconds, and launches affected players, causing no more than [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **7.5** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) when they fall. It will eventually dissipate if stalled for long enough. Despite acting as a punishment for loitering in the [Sleepy Gloom](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sleepy_Gloom), it is capable of following [Scouts](Scout.md) to the surface.

The Big Ghost can be burned up by a [Belltower](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Belltower) if it is rung, or by using a lit [![](../../images/Candlestick.png)](Candlestick.md) [Candlestick](Candlestick.md), [![](../../images/Faerie_Lantern.png)](Faerie%20Lantern.md) [Faerie Lantern](Faerie%20Lantern.md), or [![](../../images/Portable_Stove.png)](Portable%20Stove.md) [Portable Stove](Portable%20Stove.md), awarding the [![Exorcist Badge](../../images/Exorcist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge) [Exorcist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge) to all players within **32 meters.**

## Tips

- An audio cue will occur when it spawns.
- The Big Ghost CAN appear on Tenderfoot.
- The Big Ghost will stay still for a short time before exploding, allowing you to escape if you are quick enough. The [Gloom Water](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Gloom_Water) can make fleeing difficult, so it is often wise to return to the nearest dry ground or lilypad as soon as the ghost spawns and await it there. Once the ghost turns red and its expression changes, quickly run or jump away to escape the explosion radius. If there is no ground nearby, using a [![](../../images/Rescue_Claw.png)](Rescue%20Claw.md) [Rescue Claw](Rescue%20Claw.md) can be very useful.
- Using items like the [![](../../images/Fortified_Milk.png)](Fortified%20Milk.md) [Fortified Milk](Fortified%20Milk.md) or [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Tenacity.png)](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) [Scout's Tenacity](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) to gain [Invincibility](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Invincibility) right before the explosion can prevent gaining any [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) or [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury).
- The [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) will decay faster than you accumulate [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) in the [Sleepy Gloom](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sleepy_Gloom), so on high stamina it serves more to disorient and delay.
- The Big Ghost may most easily be stalled out by going onto a large island and running circles around the edge, being sure to stay just out of its reach. If no islands are available, the next best option (if you don't want to use any items) is to back yourself into a corner to avoid being launched.

## Trivia

- The Big Ghost can be burned by a lit [Campfire](Campfire.md), despite it being virtually impossible for it to encounter one.
