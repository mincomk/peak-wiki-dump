The **Scout's Generosity** is a [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. As an [amulet](Amulets.md), it can be obtained from one of the [Amulet Statues](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Amulet_Statues) found across the mountain. On use, it duplicates an item sitting close enough to be highlighted. When duplicating an item, the user either gains [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) **25** [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) for a normal item or [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) **45** [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) for a mystical item.

[Amulets](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Amulet),[![](../../images/Scout_Effigy.png)](Scout%20Effigy.md) [Scout Effigies](Scout%20Effigy.md) and [![](../../images/Checkpoint_Flag.png)](Checkpoint%20Flag.md) [Checkpoint Flags](Checkpoint%20Flag.md) cannot be duplicated by the Scout's Generosity.

Scout's Generosity cannot be [cooked](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cooked).

## Other uses

It can be bestowed upon the sash of the Scout Statue behind the [Peak](Peak%20%28biome%29.md) along with the other three Scout's Items to transform the [![](../../images/Strange_Gem.png)](Strange%20Gem.md) [Strange Gem](Strange%20Gem.md) into [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Honor.png)](Scout%27s%20Honor.md) [Scout's Honor](Scout%27s%20Honor.md).

## Tips

- Duplicated items will have the same number of uses available as the original item, so it is advisable to wait until afterwards to use an item if planning to duplicate it.
- Since [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) **20** [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) is removed upon lighting a campfire, it is strictly beneficial to spread out your uses across Scouts to maximize cloning capabilities. This lets you potentially reach up to 4 cloned items per biome, with only [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) **5** [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) as a drawback.
- Since all similar type items inflict the same amount of [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify), it's important to use it on superior items to maximize value. For example, choose the [![](../../images/Cursed_Skull.png)](Cursed%20Skull.md) [Cursed Skull](Cursed%20Skull.md) over the [![](../../images/Ritual_Dagger.png)](Ritual%20Dagger.md) [Ritual Dagger](Ritual%20Dagger.md) and [![](../../images/Scout_Cookies.png)](Scout%20Cookies.md) [Scout Cookies](Scout%20Cookies.md) over [![](../../images/Airline_Food.png)](Airline%20Food.md) [Airline Food](Airline%20Food.md).
- How useful this Amulet is depends greatly on the current [Ascent](Ascents.md). On lower difficulties and on the standard gamemode,[![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) is generally not a concern if the player is moving at a decent rate. However, on higher Ascents (especially 6 and beyond), where pausing for even just a moment can cause critical damage to a run, being able to duplicate an item like cooked Scout Cookies can easily save a climb.

## Interpretation

Each [amulet](Amulets.md) and its respective ability embodies a particular trait that [Scoutmaster Myres](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md)' troop had. To be generous is to be *"characterized by a noble or kindly spirit."* One who is generous may give away possessions out of the kindness of their heart. In this context, bringing more items to the group could be interpreted as showing such kindness and therefore generosity. From a philosophical perspective, one could imagine each [amulet](Amulets.md) to greatly intensify the already present traits within the [player character](Scout.md) to allow to achieve otherwise impossible feats rather than to directly have supernatural abilities.
