The **Ancient Idol** is a [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It is found inside of the [Mesa](Mesa.md)'s [Tomb](Tomb.md) after completing the challenge chamber.

Although the Ancient Idol cannot be found inside [Ancient Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage) or obtained from [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md), it is still considered mystical and grants the [![Esoterica Badge](../../images/Esoterica_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Esoterica_Badge) [Esoterica Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Esoterica_Badge) upon picking it up.

Actively holding the idol grants the player [![Invincibility](../../images/Status_Invincibility.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) [Invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility).

Throwing the Ancient Idol into the lava of [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md) or the lava cauldron in [The Citadel](The%20Citadel.md) destroys the idol and unlocks the [![24 Karat Badge](../../images/24_Karat_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/24_Karat_Badge) [24 Karat Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/24_Karat_Badge).

The Ancient Idol cannot be cooked.

## Tips & Tricks

The Ancient Idol does not travel very far when thrown. However, when placed inside of a [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md), the idol can be thrown much farther. This can make transporting the heavy idol much more manageable.

## Trivia

- The Ancient Idol is a golden recreation of [![](../../images/Bing_Bong.png)](Bing%20Bong.md) [Bing Bong](Bing%20Bong.md) standing atop a small pedestal.
- The Ancient Idol is the heaviest item in the game.
- During the [2025 Holiday Season](1.50.a.md), the Ancient Idol wore a Santa hat that was not golden.
