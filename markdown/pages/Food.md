**Food** in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* can be consumed to reduce [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) or gain [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina). Some foods are [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [poisonous](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), while others have additional effects, such as healing [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) or removing [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat).

Players can forage for various [natural foods](#Natural_food), such as fruits and [mushrooms](#Mushrooms), or find [packaged food](#Packaged_food) in [Luggage](Luggage.md).

Food can be [cooked](Cooking.md) to double the amount of [hunger](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Hunger) restored and increase its [bonus stamina](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bonus_stamina).

The tables below list every food item obtainable in the game, including their nutritional properties, weight, additional effects, and other notes.

## Natural food

Natural food can be found in almost every [biome](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome). It can be used to earn the [![Naturalist Badge](../../images/Naturalist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Naturalist_Badge) [Naturalist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Naturalist_Badge). [Mystical](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical) items, such as the [![](../../images/Cure-All.png)](Cure-All.md) [Cure-All](Cure-All.md) and [![](../../images/Pandora%27s_Lunchbox.png)](Pandora%27s%20Lunchbox.md) [Pandora's Lunchbox](Pandora%27s%20Lunchbox.md), are also safe for the badge.
