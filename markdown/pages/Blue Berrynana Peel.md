The **Blue Berrynana Peel** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that is acquired after eating [![](../../images/Blue_Berrynana.png)](Blue%20Berrynana.md) [Blue Berrynanas](Blue%20Berrynana.md) from the [Tropics](Tropics.md). Players walking over a peel will [slip](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Slippery_effect) and fall to the ground, losing control for a moment.
