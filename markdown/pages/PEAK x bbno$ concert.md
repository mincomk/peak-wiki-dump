**PEAK x bbno$ concert** was a collaboration event between *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* and Canadian rapper [bbno$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bbno$). In patch [1.48.a](1.48.a.md), a marble statue of bbno$ was added next to the [Crash Site](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Crash_Site) in the [Shore](Shore.md). In the following patch ([1.49.a](1.49.a.md)) a bugle was added in the statue's hands. Playing the bugle provided full morale boost and started the concert, lasting around 8 minutes featuring 3 songs by bbno$:

### Song list

- "1-800" (ft. ironmouse)
- "two"
- "bing bong" (ft. Vanillamace)

## Trivia

- The bugle, called "Bugle?", held by the statue shared the inventory icon with [![](../../images/Scoutmaster%27s_Bugle.png)](Scoutmaster%27s%20Bugle.md) [Scoutmaster's Bugle](Scoutmaster%27s%20Bugle.md) but was technically a new item.- The 3D model had a distinct color and played a unique sound effect.
  - Like the Scoutmaster's Bugle, it provided a [Morale Boost](Morale%20Boost.md) for [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **100** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) when played, and could unlock the [![Animal Serenading Badge](../../images/Animal_Serenading_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) [Animal Serenading Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) if played at the [Oasis](Mesa.md#Oasis) or [Hot Spring](Alpine.md#Hot_Spring).
- The bugle could be played in any biome for audible music and the Morale Boost. The giant performers were only visible from the [Shore](Shore.md), but the visual effects would appear up until the [Caldera](Caldera.md) and the multicolored sky would persist from the end of the last song until the lighting of the next biome's [Campfire](Campfire.md).- If the bugle was played in or shortly before entering the [Mesa](Mesa.md), the sun would be prevented from inflicting [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) damage while the colorful sky effects were present.
- Pressing ![Keyboard 7.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_7.png/25px-Keyboard_7.png?50b22a) (seven) on the keyboard seven times while in the [Airport](Airport.md) unlocked ironmouse's wig, a secret [Hat](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Hat) cosmetic. The hat would disappear from the [Passport](Passport.md) upon quitting the game but it would remain equipped.- The hat used the [Strange Gem](Strange%20Gem.md) as a placeholder icon.
- [Bing Bong](Bing%20Bong.md) is bbno$'s biggest fan.
