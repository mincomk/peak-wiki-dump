The **Tick** is a natural [food](Food.md) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. They have a 1% chance to spawn on a [Scout](Scout.md) as they walk through [Monstera](Tropics.md#Monstera) plants in the [Tropics](Tropics.md) and will inflict up to [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **50** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) over time until removed. Once removed, it can be eaten to restore [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **20** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger).

While latched onto a Scout, it will grow overtime. The size of the tick has no effect on how much Hunger it restores.

As with other bug-like creatures, turning on [Bug Phobia Mode](Bug%20Phobia%20Mode.md) in the [Settings](Settings.md) will turn the Tick into its [Bing Bong](Bing%20Bong.md) variant.

## Tips

- If you are slowly taking small increments of [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) damage overtime without reason, a Tick has likely latched onto you.
- It is ideal to let another Scout remove the Tick for you. If you are playing solo, a good way to remove the Tick yourself is by flicking the camera left and right until you can select it.
- If you are willing to let it poison you for an extended period of time, you can preserve a Tick on you to later consume it for extra food as you can only receive [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **50** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) maximum. It also does not increase [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) while attached.

## Trivia

- The Tick has no limit on how big it can grow, able to become larger than the Scout it is leeching off of and beyond if you let it.
- If you make it to the [PEAK](Peak%20%28biome%29.md) while a Tick is attached to you, it will not appear after you board the helicopter.
