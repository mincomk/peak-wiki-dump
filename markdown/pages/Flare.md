The **Flare** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It can be found at the [crash site](Shore.md#Crash_Site), in [Luggage](Luggage.md), and in a small crate full of them at the [PEAK](Peak%20%28biome%29.md) while not doing [Ascent 4+](Ascents.md).

A flare can be lit to display a tall column of colourful smoke matching the colour of the scout who uses it. Lighting a flare at the [PEAK](Peak%20%28biome%29.md) signals the rescue helicopter, completing the expedition. Once a flare is lit, it can no longer be stored in the player's inventory or in a [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a flare causes it to explode, dealing up to [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **20** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) to scouts within **4.8 meters**, depending on distance.
