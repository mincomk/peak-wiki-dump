The **Fanny Pack** is an [equipment](Equipment.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It is equipped in the fourth equipment slot, similar to the [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md). It allows [scouts](Scout.md) to carry two more items during their climb.

The Fanny Pack cannot spawn in a solo expedition. The **Fanny Pack** is quite rare and can be found in all luggages except the [Clown Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Clown_Luggage).
