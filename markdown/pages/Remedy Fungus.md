The **Remedy Fungus** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. When dropped or thrown, it releases an explosion that immediately heals players within **8 meters** for [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **17.5** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) with an additional [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **35** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), and [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) removed over the course of **14 seconds** if they remain in the area of effect.

## Tips

- The Remedy Fungus can be used to negate [fall damage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Fall_damage). The healing cloud will remove a certain amount of [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) when the item is dropped, either manually by the player before landing, or automatically upon [losing consciousness](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Death) (provided the Remedy Fungus was in one of the inventory slots).
