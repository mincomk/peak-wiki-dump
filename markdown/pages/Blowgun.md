The **Blowgun** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in regular and big [luggages](Luggage.md) while playing multiplayer. It can be used to shoot a dart at another [Scout](Scout.md) to clear all statuses except [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) and [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify).

The Blowgun causes the shot Scout to fall asleep due to them receiving [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **120** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy). This will prevent earning the [![Survivalist Badge](../../images/Survivalist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivalist_Badge) [Survivalist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Survivalist_Badge), as the Scout will invariably lose consciousness.

([Cooking](Cooking.md) a blowgun causes it to inflict [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **150** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) instead of [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **120** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy). Additionally, the user gains [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **10** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina).) This is currently bugged and gives [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **120** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) even when cooked, along with [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **10** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina).
