# Roots

The **Roots** is one of the two possible second [biomes](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* (the other being the [Tropics](Tropics.md)). It follows the [Shore](Shore.md) and precedes either the [Alpine](Alpine.md) or the [Mesa](Mesa.md), depending on the map [seed](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Seed). It is comprised of a redwood forest infested with mushrooms.

Reaching the top and lighting the [campfire](Campfire.md) dispels the fog wall blocking access to the next biome and grants all living [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) the [![Forestry Badge](../../images/Forestry_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Forestry_Badge) [Forestry Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Forestry_Badge) if they haven't already obtained it.

When playing on the [Peak Ascent](Ascents.md) or higher, the [Fog](Fog.md) will eventually start rising in this biome.

## Features

### Redwood Trees

---

[![Roots redwood tree.png](../../images/Roots_redwood_tree.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_redwood_tree.png)

**Redwood Trees** with thick trunks and conical crowns appear in the Roots. Small natural shelf fungi can be found scattered upon their trunks. They often feature wooden platforms you can stand on, and can have [![](../../images/Bounce_Fungus.png)](Bounce%20Fungus.md) [Bounce Fungi](Bounce%20Fungus.md) [![](../../images/Cloud_Fungus.png)](Cloud%20Fungus.md) [Cloud Fungi](Cloud%20Fungus.md), and [![](../../images/Shelf_Fungus.png)](Shelf%20Fungus.md) [Shelf Fungi](Shelf%20Fungus.md) to pick up. The trees' canopies are standable locations, which can be useful for traversal.

### Redwood Stumps

---

- [![Roots stump.png](../../images/Roots_stump.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_stump.png)
- [![Hollow trunk honeycomb.jpg](../../images/Hollow_trunk_honeycomb.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AHollow_trunk_honeycomb.jpg)

**Redwood Stumps** appear all around the Roots. Some of these stumps are hollow, with an entrance at the bottom and top. These hollow stumps may contain a [![](../../images/Honeycomb.png)](Honeycomb.md) [Honeycomb](Honeycomb.md).

### Vines

---

[![Roots vines.png](../../images/Roots_vines.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_vines.png)

**Vines** can be found throughout the Roots that connect various points on the mountain. They can be grappled onto similar to [rope](Rope.md), taking very little [stamina](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina) while doing so. Moving along the vines takes some stamina, but much less than climbing. Hanging on a vine without moving drains stamina at a very slow rate.

[Shroomberries](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Shroomberries) can sometimes be found growing on vines.

### Giant Shelf Fungi

---

[![Roots shelf fungus.png](../../images/Roots_shelf_fungus.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_shelf_fungus.png)

**Giant Shelf Fungi** can be found growing on the trees and on the climb in the Roots. They act as platforms to help scouts maneuver the biome.

### Bouncy Mushrooms

---

[![Roots red shroom.jpg](../../images/Roots_red_shroom.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_red_shroom.jpg)

**Bouncy Mushrooms** can be found all throughout the Roots. Landing on the top of one will bounce a scout and restore a small amount of stamina, identical to a [![](../../images/Bounce_Fungus.png)](Bounce%20Fungus.md) [Bounce Fungus](Bounce%20Fungus.md).

### Water

---

[![Roots water.png](../../images/Roots_water.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_water.png)

**Water** can be found covering the lowest parts of the Roots. Scouts' movement speed is reduced while wading through the water.

### Waterfalls

---

[![Roots waterfall.png](../../images/Roots_waterfall.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARoots_waterfall.png)

**Waterfalls** can also spawn in the Roots, similar to those seen in the [Tropics](Tropics.md). The water may make it harder to see the terrain behind it, but otherwise waterfalls are completely benign. Touching the water will knock a [Scout](Scout.md) over for a couple of seconds without doing any damage.

## Tips & tricks

- The trees are often not connected well, so traversing along the floor is advised.
- [Zombies](Zombie.md) only activate while you are in their line of sight. If you see one, run away before it sees you.
- Climbing giant redwoods near the cliff face can get you very high before getting onto the face.
- Similar to [Blizzards](Blizzard.md) in the [Alpine](Alpine.md), wind is less dangerous when rock formations or trees are used as cover. Running against the wind or grabbing onto nearby surfaces can help prevent your [Scout](Scout.md) from getting blown off from a high place or into a hazard.
- It is wise to wait until wind has just passed before attempting lengthy or risky maneuvers (i.e. crossing bridges between redwood tree platforms).
- Due to the high number of entity hazards in the biome, maintaining awareness is important. Audio cues can alert players to the presence of nearby [Spiders](Spider.md) or [Beetles](Beetle.md), as well as signalling when a player is being targeted by an active [zombie](Zombie.md).
- It is often impossible to find a route completely unobstructed by spore clouds. As a result, [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) can accumulate quickly and in large amounts while in the Roots, and the [status effect](Stamina%20bar.md) is among the slowest-decaying in the game. [![](../../images/Fortified_Milk.png)](Fortified%20Milk.md) [Fortified Milk](Fortified%20Milk.md) is helpful to mitigate the accumulation of [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) when moving through particularly difficult areas. Most healing items also remove [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), with the exception of [![](../../images/Bandages.png)](Bandages.md) [Bandages](Bandages.md).
