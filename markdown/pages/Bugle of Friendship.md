The **Bugle of Friendship** is a mystical [equipment](Equipment.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that can be found while playing in multiplayer. While a Scout plays the bugle, all other players within a **56 meter** radius will gain **0.5 seconds** of [![Infinite Stamina](../../images/Status_Infinite_Stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) [Infinite Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina). The bugle has a total use time of **7.5 seconds**. This makes it an amazing item for helping teammates cross large vertical distances.

You can receive the [![Animal Serenading Badge](../../images/Animal_Serenading_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) [Animal Serenading Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) by using the Bugle of Friendship while standing next to a [capybara](Capybara.md), although using the regular [![](../../images/Bugle.png)](Bugle.md) [Bugle](Bugle.md) for this is less wasteful. The badge will still be unlocked if the Bugle of Friendship is used without any use time left.
