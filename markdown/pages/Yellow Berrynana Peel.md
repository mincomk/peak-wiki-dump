The **Yellow Berrynana Peel** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that is acquired after eating [![](../../images/Yellow_Berrynana.png)](Yellow%20Berrynana.md) [Yellow Berrynanas](Yellow%20Berrynana.md) from the [Tropics](Tropics.md) or the [Gloom](Gloom.md). Players walking over a peel will [slip](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Slippery_effect) and fall to the ground, losing control for a moment. It can also be obtained on its own in [Clown Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Clown_Luggage).
