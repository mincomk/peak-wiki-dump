# Blizzard

[![](../../images/Blizzard_2.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ABlizzard_2.jpg)

A Blizzard

**Blizzards** are environmental hazards exclusive to the [Alpine](Alpine.md). They happen at random times and last several seconds. Any players not properly sheltered will begin receiving significant [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold). Additionally, blizzards severely reduce the visibility and have winds that constantly push you away. They aren't as aggressive as the wind in [Roots](Roots.md), however, they do slightly push you.

The game determines whether the players are sheltered based on the light level, with darker areas providing more protection.[[1]](#cite_note-1) The players also have to be inside an invisible volume to be affected by the blizzard. Being surrounded by objects, cliffs, or other [Scouts](Scout.md) does not affect the intensity of the [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold).

[Huddling](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Huddling) with other players can help mitigate the [Cold](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cold) gained from being exposed to a blizzard. [Gripping](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Climbing) onto any climbable surface will also anchor you to combat the [Wind](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Wind)[[2]](#cite_note-2) like in [Roots](Roots.md).
