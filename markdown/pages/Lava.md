**Lava** in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* is an environmental hazard which deals [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) damage and [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury). It appears in large pools in the [Caldera](Caldera.md) and [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md), and in the form of [Lava Tubes](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava_Tube) in the [Tropics](Tropics.md) and [Alpine](Alpine.md).

Pools of lava can [cook](#Cooking) items that are sufficiently close.

## Variations

### Caldera

The lava in the [Caldera](Caldera.md) follows a **71.75-second** tidal cycle. The rocks visually change their appearance as the lava gets closer, though this is a purely cosmetic effect since the damage is defined by the player's distance to the lava's surface.

### The Kiln

[The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md)'s lava will start to rise **3 minutes** after lighting the [campfire](Campfire.md), and will take **20 minutes** to rise to the top of the volcano.

### Lava rivers

**Lava rivers** (also known as *lava tubes*) may occasionally spawn in the [Tropics](Tropics.md), [Alpine](Alpine.md), and [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md).

Despite their appearance, lava rivers do not share physical properties with lava found in pools. They can also be climbed, and [items](Items.md) do not sink in them.

## Damage mechanics

Players in direct contact with the [Caldera](Caldera.md)'s or [Kiln](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Kiln)'s lava receive [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **25** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) and [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **25** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) every second. Depending on the distance to the lava's surface, players may take up to [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **2.5** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) every ~0.208 seconds[[1]](#cite_note-1).

Lava rivers deal [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **20** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) and [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **50** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) and apply a small amount of knockback to all players directly touching them.

## Cooking

Pools of lava can [cook](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cook) nearby items at a rate depending on their distance to the lava, with the minimum time of ~1.43 seconds. Items in equipped [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpacks](Backpack.md) are not cooked by lava.
