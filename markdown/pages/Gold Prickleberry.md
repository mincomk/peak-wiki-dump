The **Gold Prickleberry** is a [natural food](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Natural_food) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found growing on [Cacti](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cacti) in the [Mesa](Mesa.md). Eating it restores [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **15** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger), [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **10** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat), heals [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **10** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) and attaches 2 thorns to the Scout's head, inflicting [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) **10** [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns). Because the thorns are attached to the head, you cannot remove them from yourself.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Gold Prickleberry removes the [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) effect, in addition to the normal effects of cooking.
