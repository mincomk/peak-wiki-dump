The **Ritual Dagger** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) [Mystical](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It can be obtained from [Ancient Luggages](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage) or an [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md) when playing co-op. The dagger can be used by "sacrificing" another Scout from behind, killing that Scout and healing the user (in a small AOE) of most negative status effects.

The Ritual Dagger can be [cooked](Cooking.md) but its functionality or effects don't change.

Removing more than 75% negative status effects in one use grants the user the [![Last Resort Badge](../../images/Last_Resort_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Last_Resort_Badge) [Last Resort Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Last_Resort_Badge).

## Usage Mechanics

To use the dagger, the following conditions must be met:

- The victim must face away from the user.
- The victim must appear as a Scout and not as a [cooked chicken](Cannibalism.md).

Once used, the following happens:

- The victim's [Bonus Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_Stamina) bar gets completely filled with [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify), killing them immediately.
- The user of the dagger is cured of most negative status effects.- This does not include [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight), [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns), [![Arrow](../../images/Status_Arrow.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Arrow) [Arrow](Stamina%20bar.md#Arrow)[[confirmation needed](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Category%3APages_needing_confirmation)], [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse), or [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify).
  - The user also gains [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **100** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina).

## Notes

- Despite appearing and behaving similarly to Scouts, [Zombies](Zombie.md) cannot be sacrificed.
