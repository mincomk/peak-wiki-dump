The **Jetpack** is an wearable [Equipment](Equipment.md) [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It takes the fourth equipment slot similar to the [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md), and you cannot wear both at the same time. Each Jetpack starts fully fueled. While in mid-air, pressing or holding ![Keyboard space.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_space.png/25px-Keyboard_space.png?9ad9c4) **Space** will activate the Jetpack and slowly ascend the Scout into the air, consuming **20%** of the fuel every second.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) the Jetpack has no effect.
