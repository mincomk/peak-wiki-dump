The **Cactus** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in the [Mesa](Mesa.md). A Cactus will stick to the player and will apply [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) **20** [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) until thrown or picked off the player's body. It must be thrown (40% or higher throw power) and cannot be dropped due to its sticky attribute.

Having 5 or more Cacti stuck to the player grants the [![Needlepoint Badge](../../images/Needlepoint_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Needlepoint_Badge) [Needlepoint Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Needlepoint_Badge). Held Cacti do not count towards this total.

[Prickleberries](Prickleberry.md) do not grow on small Cacti.
