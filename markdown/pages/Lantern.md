The **Lantern** is an [equipment](Equipment.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. One lantern can be found in the [Crash Site](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Crash_site), with more lanterns potentially being found in [luggages](Luggage.md) in the [Tropics](Tropics.md) and [Alpine](Alpine.md). While lit, all scouts within **7.2 meters** of the lantern recover [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **2.5** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) each second. The Lantern can be lit for up to **60 seconds** before running out of fuel.

A Lantern will stay lit when dropped or when placed into a dropped backpack. It is automatically extinguished if it is put away, if the containing backpack is picked up, or if it is taken out of a containing backpack.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a lantern while it has remaining fuel will cause it to explode, dealing up to [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **20** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) to scouts within **4.8 meters**, depending on their distance.
