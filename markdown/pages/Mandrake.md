The **Mandrake** is a natural [food](Food.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It has a 28.57% chance to spawn instead of a [![](../../images/Medicinal_Root.png)](Medicinal%20Root.md) [Medicinal Root](Medicinal%20Root.md) in [Roots](Roots.md). Until uprooted, it looks identical to a Medicinal Root.

Once uprooted and near a conscious player, the Mandrake will scream loudly for **3 seconds**, followed by a cooldown of **0.75-2 seconds**. While it is screaming, players within **16 meters** gain [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **5** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) every quarter second. [Cooking](Cooking.md) the Mandrake will stop the screaming. The Mandrake cannot be stored in a player's inventory slots, but it can be stored in a backpack.

Eating (or being fed) a [cooked](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cooked) Mandrake grants the player the [![Cryptogastronomy Badge](../../images/Cryptogastronomy_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge) [Cryptogastronomy Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge) and removes [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) **25** [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse). Cooking the Mandrake stops it from screaming.

Eating (or being fed) a raw Mandrake applies the [numb](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Numb) effect to the player for **60 seconds**.

Stamina bar hidden by the [Numb](Stamina%20bar.md#Numb) effect.

## Tips

- If the [![Cryptogastronomy Badge](../../images/Cryptogastronomy_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge) [Cryptogastronomy Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge) has been completed then players should quickly throw the Mandrake away, as the scream it produces builds up [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) quickly.- If the goal is to complete the [![Cryptogastronomy Badge](../../images/Cryptogastronomy_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge) [Cryptogastronomy Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cryptogastronomy_Badge), then having a [![](../../images/Portable_Stove.png)](Portable%20Stove.md) [Portable Stove](Portable%20Stove.md) is almost a necessity, as players cannot store the Mandrake outside of a [backpack](Backpack.md), and even then it continues to scream.
- After using [![](../../images/The_Book_of_Bones.png)](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md) [The Book of Bones](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md), you are immune to the [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) the Mandrake normally provides, allowing you to safely carry it. However, be careful around other players!

## Trivia

- If the player's sound effects volume is set to 0, they won't be affected by the Mandrake's scream drowsiness effect.
