The **Antidote** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. An Antidote can be used up to 3 times, removing [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **35** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **35** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **10** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) and poison over time effects with each use.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) or throwing an antidote will cause it to shatter, removing up to [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **20** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) and [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) from players within **4.8 meters** depending on their distance. This does not remove any existing poison over time effects that have not elapsed. Throwing an antidote directly at a player however will bonk them, causing them to enter a [ragdoll](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragdoll) state.

Due to [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) mostly occurring in the [Tropics](Tropics.md) and [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) only appearing in the [Roots](Roots.md), Antidotes can only spawn in luggages in these biomes.

## Tips

- As a thrown Antidote removes [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **20** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) and [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) from players regardless of how many times it was used it is ideal to throw a near-empty bottle directly on the ground where you and your poisoned/spored friends stand to make the most of it. This is pointless in a solo run due to the decreased amount of restoration however.
- This item is **essential** when confronting [Zombies](Zombie.md), as it is one of the ways to remove the zombie infection, as well as having multiple charges to do so.- You may also throw the bottle at a Zombie to bonk it, causing it to ragdoll as an emergency option.
