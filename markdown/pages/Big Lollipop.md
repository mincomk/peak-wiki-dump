The **Big Lollipop** is a packaged [food](Food.md) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in regular, big, and explorer's [luggages](Luggage.md). A smaller unusable version can also be found inside the [Airport](Airport.md)'s [photobooth](Airport.md#Photobooth).

Consuming a Big Lollipop grants you [![Infinite Stamina](../../images/Status_Infinite_Stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) [Infinite Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) for **11 seconds**, with the duration being shortened to **8 seconds** when you climb. This means you can wait **3 seconds** to begin climbing and still get the full **8 seconds** of climbing with infinite stamina. After the effect ends, the player will gain [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **32.5** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) over the course of **10 seconds**. Cooking a Big Lollipop removes this penalty.
