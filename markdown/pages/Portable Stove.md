The **Portable Stove** is an [deployable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Deployable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in [luggages](Luggage.md) anywhere outside of the [Shore](Shore.md). It can be placed down, allowing you to cook items and removing [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **5** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) per second from players within **3.2 meters**.. The Portable Stove extinguishes itself after 1 minute.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Portable Stove causes it to explode, dealing up to [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **20** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) to scouts within within **4.8 meters**, depending on their distance.
