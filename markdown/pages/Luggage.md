# Luggage

**Luggage boxes** are item containers scattered across the island. Their locations are determined by the map seed and remain fixed for the duration of the current map. The contents of each box are randomly generated upon opening.

The amount and type of items inside a luggage depends on its type, and for [Regular](#Regular_Luggage) and [Big Luggage](#Big_Luggage), the biome they're found in. Items inside the boxes are unmovable until picked up.

Opening 15 luggage boxes in a single run unlocks the [![Plunderer Badge](../../images/Plunderer_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Plunderer_Badge) [Plunderer Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Plunderer_Badge).

## Types

There are six types of luggage boxes, out of which five contain items.

All item spawn chances are rounded to one decimal.

### Regular Luggage

---

[![Regular Luggage.png](../../images/Regular_Luggage.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARegular_Luggage.png)

**Regular Luggage** is the most common luggage box, it's brown and moderately sized. Regular Luggage always contains one item, and has a 1 in 3 chance to contain another. The item spawn pool differs slightly based on the biome the it's found in. One Regular Luggage is guaranteed to spawn at the [Crash Site](Shore.md#Crash_Site).

### Big Luggage

---

[![Big Luggage.png](../../images/Big_Luggage.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ABig_Luggage.png)

**Big Luggage** is a less common luggage box, with an appearance similar to the Regular Luggage, but slightly taller and white in color. The Big Luggage contains 2 items and has a 1 in 3 chance to contain another. It has the same spawn pools as the Regular Luggage. One Big Luggage is guaranteed to spawn at the [Crash Site](Shore.md#Crash_Site).

**Clown Luggage** is a very big, rare luggage featuring a colorful design and decorations resembling a clown face. The box always holds 6 items. All items besides [![](../../images/Balloon.png)](Balloon.md) [Balloons](Balloon.md) and [![](../../images/Balloon_Bunch.png)](Balloon%20Bunch.md) [Balloon Bunches](Balloon%20Bunch.md) will be ejected out of the luggage upon opening it.

The [![](../../images/Yellow_Berrynana_Peel.png)](Yellow%20Berrynana%20Peel.md) [Yellow Berrynana Peels](Yellow%20Berrynana%20Peel.md), which are almost always among the contents, can be dangerous when combined with the potential to find lit [![](../../images/Dynamite.png)](Dynamite.md) [Dynamite](Dynamite.md) within.

Opening two Clown Luggages in a single run unlocks the [![Jester Badge](../../images/Jester_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Jester_Badge) [Jester Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Jester_Badge).

### Luggage Trap

---

[![Mimic Luggage.png](../../images/Mimic_Luggage.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMimic_Luggage.png)
[![Mimic Luggage Open.png](../../images/Mimic_Luggage_Open.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMimic_Luggage_Open.png)

The **Luggage Trap** is a rare luggage-shaped trap that spawns in [The Citadel](The%20Citadel.md). It contains no items and upon opening a burst of gas is released, inflicting [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **5** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) every **0.5 seconds** to any scouts within **16 meters**.

The Luggage Trap has an initial appearance of a [Regular Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Regular_Luggage), but with some subtle differences; the trap luggage is slightly open and its handle is pointed upwards, unlike the handles on other luggages. Inside it has teeth and purple lining, as opposed to the green lining of the Regular Luggage.
