# Big Egg

Big Egg
[![Big Egg.png](../../images/Big_Egg.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ABig_Egg.png)
[Item type](Items.md)- [Misc](Misc.md)
Biome- [Caldera](Caldera.md)
Weight
[![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) **5**

The **Big Egg** is an [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in nests on rocks in the [Caldera](Caldera.md), including those patrolled by [Condors](Condor.md).

[![](../../images/Caldera_nest.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ACaldera_nest.png)

Two Big Eggs in a nest in [Caldera](Caldera.md).

A Big Egg can be cracked by throwing it to make an [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) or, with a 10% chance, a [![](../../images/Cooked_Bird.png)](Cooked%20Bird.md) [Cooked Bird](Cooked%20Bird.md). Cooking an [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) is required for the [![Gourmand Badge](../../images/Gourmand_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Gourmand_Badge) [Gourmand Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Gourmand_Badge), as the [![](../../images/Cooked_Bird.png)](Cooked%20Bird.md) [Cooked Bird](Cooked%20Bird.md) will not count.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Big Egg will transfer its cooking level to the item inside (with the exception of a would-be uncooked bird).

## Trivia

- Paradoxically, a Big Egg is lighter than the [![](../../images/Cooked_Bird.png)](Cooked%20Bird.md) [Cooked Bird](Cooked%20Bird.md) that may be inside it (Cooked Bird's [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) **10** [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) vs. Big Egg's [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) **5** [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight)).
- Throwing a Big Egg at a wall causes the produced [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) to stick to that wall. The egg item does not inherit this property.
- Throwing a Big Egg on top of a player causes the produced [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Egg](Egg.md) to float, allowing players to use it as a platform.
- Although impractical, a Big Egg can also be cracked by throwing other items at it.
