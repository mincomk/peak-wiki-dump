The **Scoutmaster's Bugle** is a [mystical](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical) [equipment](Equipment.md) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It has an extremely rare chance of being obtained from [Ancient Luggage](Luggage.md#Ancient_Luggage) or an [Ancient Statue](Ancient%20Statue.md).

Blowing the bugle will produce a distorted noise, drastically different than the standard [Bugle](Bugle.md). The Scoutmaster's Bugle can only be used once.

When consumed, it will grant a [Morale Boost](Morale%20Boost.md) to the user and all other [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts), granting [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **100** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina). It will also summon [The Scoutmaster](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md), who will mark the bugle's user as his target. The Scoutmaster doesn't appear on Tenderfoot difficulty.

The Scoutmaster will chase his target for **2 minutes** before despawning, ignoring his normal despawn conditions.

If the Scoutmaster's Bugle is incinerated, it will inflict [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) on the user when played, in the same way as consuming incinerated [Food](Food.md).

Despite not naturally spawning there, the Scoutmaster's Bugle will still spawn The Scoutmaster in [Nadir](Nadir.md).

Playing a Scoutmaster's Bugle while standing next to a [capybara](Capybara.md) grants the user the [![Animal Serenading Badge](../../images/Animal_Serenading_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) [Animal Serenading Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge). The bugle does not need to be consumed to unlock the badge.
