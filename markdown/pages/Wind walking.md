# Wind walking

![](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/Stub.svg?ddd5ba)

This page is a [stub](https://support.wiki.gg/wiki/Stub). You can help **PEAK Wiki** by expanding it.

**Wind walking** is a temporary player state in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* that produces a low-gravity movement effect. It is gained after activating [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Initiative.png)](Scout%27s%20Initiative.md) [Scout's Initiative](Scout%27s%20Initiative.md) and occasionally by eating a [![](../../images/Red_Shroomberry.png)](Red%20Shroomberry.md) [Red Shroomberry](Red%20Shroomberry.md), [![](../../images/Yellow_Shroomberry.png)](Yellow%20Shroomberry.md) [Yellow Shroomberry](Yellow%20Shroomberry.md), or (more rarely) a [![](../../images/Purple_Shroomberry.png)](Purple%20Shroomberry.md) [Purple Shroomberry](Purple%20Shroomberry.md).

While wind walking is active, the player experiences lighter, floatier physics, which affects jumps and falls. The effect lasts for 3 seconds after activating [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Initiative.png)](Scout%27s%20Initiative.md) [Scout's Initiative](Scout%27s%20Initiative.md), and for 15 seconds, beginning 3 seconds after when eating a [Shroomberry](Shroomberry.md).

If a [Scout](Scout.md) has 3 or more [![](../../images/Balloon.png)](Balloon.md) [Balloons](Balloon.md) equipped while the wind walking is active, they will begin floating up.
