# Big Cacti

Not to be confused with a smaller [![](../../images/Cactus.png)](Cactus.md) [Cactus](Cactus.md) also found in the [Mesa](Mesa.md).
Big Cacti
[![Big cactus.png](../../images/Big_cactus.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ABig_cactus.png)
General
Type
Environmental hazard
Biome- [Mesa](Mesa.md)

**Big Cacti** are an environmental hazard found all throughout the [Mesa](Mesa.md). Some Big Cacti are big and tall, while others are much smaller and shaped like a ball.

Touching a Big Cactus makes the player temporarily stick to it, while also inflicting [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) on them.

[![](../../images/Red_Prickleberry.png)](Red%20Prickleberry.md) [Red Prickleberry](Red%20Prickleberry.md) and [![](../../images/Gold_Prickleberry.png)](Gold%20Prickleberry.md) [Gold Prickleberry](Gold%20Prickleberry.md) can be found growing on Big Cacti.

## Tips

- Pressing the helping hand hotkey (![Mouse right.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Mouse_right.png/25px-Mouse_right.png?6d2268) **Right Click**) can get other scouts unstuck from a cactus.
