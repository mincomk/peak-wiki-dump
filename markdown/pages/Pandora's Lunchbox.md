The **Pandora's Lunchbox** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. When used, it clears all status effects besides [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) (including [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) and [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse)), then gives the player a random amount of [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger), [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina), [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat), [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy), [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), and [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse). It does not remove status over time effects, such as those gained from eating poisonous food or from a [Zombie Bite](Zombie.md) although the zombie infection will stop if the player has [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **0** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) after using Pandora's Lunchbox.

Pandora's Lunchbox can give anywhere from **0-100** total statuses, with an average amount of **40**. Half as much [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) is applied on average compared to other statuses. A player can receive anywhere from **0-100** [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) (limited by [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify)), with an average amount of **30**. The effects of Pandora's Lunchbox bypass [![Invincibility](../../images/Status_Invincibility.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) [Invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) Pandora's Lunchbox causes it to explode, applying its effects to all players within **4.8 meters**.

## Tips

- It is recommended not to use Pandora's Lunchbox while having a lot of [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) due to the fact that it will often remove much of it.
- If the player is a [skeleton](Skeleton.md), using Pandora's Lunchbox will often prove fatal due to the [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) multiplier applied to skeletons.
- As Pandora's Lunchbox can also give non-decaying statuses such as [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger), [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) or [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse), using it is a bit risky, especially on the last use. With multiple uses remaining, there are more chances to get a favorable outcome.
- If multiple scouts are in a dire need of a Pandora's Lunchbox dose and there is only one use left, gathering close and [Cooking](Cooking.md) it might be a worthy try.

## Trivia

- The symbol on the front is Evil Landfall?'s logo.
