# Airport

Airport
[![Airport Location.png](../../images/Airport_Location.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_Location.png)
Details
Type
Lobby

The **Airport** is the lobby area in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. Players are brought here when starting a new game or finishing one. It is the only [location](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Location) where players cannot receive any [status effects](Stamina%20bar.md) (except [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight)).

Players in the Airport are given a [![](../../images/Passport.png)](Passport.md) [Passport](Passport.md) which allows them to [customize](Cosmetics.md) their [Scout](Scout.md)'s appearance, including their skin color and cosmetics. A number of numerous side-activities can also be done inside the Airport while waiting for a game to start.

The Airport belongs to a fictional company called "*[BingBong](Bing%20Bong.md) Airlines*".

## Features

### Elevators

---

[![Airport elevator.png](../../images/Airport_elevator.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_elevator.png)

All players initially spawn in an elevator when entering the Airport. The elevator's doors are initially closed and open after a couple of seconds.

### Mirror

---

[![Airport mirror.png](../../images/Airport_mirror.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_mirror.png)

The mirror located on the wall opposite to the Gate Kiosk allows players to see their [Scouts](Scout.md) and their emotes.

### Photobooth

---

- [![Exterior](../../images/Airport_photo_booth_exterior.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_photo_booth_exterior.png)
  Exterior
- [![Interior](../../images/Airport_photo_booth_interior.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_photo_booth_interior.png)
  Interior

The photobooth located to the right of the mirror allows players to take images of themselves by interacting with a special Kiosk inside. Once interacted with, a **3 second** countdown will begin before taking a picture. A total of four pictures are taken at a time and when presented to the player in-game a screenshot will automatically be taken.

There are also a few items that can be picked up and interacted with on the floor of the photobooth, including one of each:

- [![](../../images/Binoculars.png)](Binoculars.md) [Binoculars](Binoculars.md) (does not switch camera to look through the lenses)
- [![](../../images/Bing_Bong.png)](Bing%20Bong.md) [Bing Bong](Bing%20Bong.md) (will face away from the player holding it)
- [![](../../images/Big_Lollipop.png)](Big%20Lollipop.md) [Big Lollipop](Big%20Lollipop.md) (cannot be licked, despite displaying the prompt to lick it)
- [![](../../images/Bugle.png)](Bugle.md) [Bugle](Bugle.md) (does not produce sound)

### Conveyors

---

[![Airport conveyor.png](../../images/Airport_conveyor.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_conveyor.png)

Two conveyor belts going in opposite directions are located in the middle of the room. [Scouts](Scout.md) standing or [ragdolling](Scout.md#Ragdoll_State) on them are slowly moved in the corresponding direction.

### Chess board

---

[![Airport chess board.png](../../images/Airport_chess_board.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_chess_board.png)

To the right of the conveyor belts is a large chess board that players may use to play chess. Two additional [![](../../images/Queen_Black.png)](Queen.md) [Queens](Queen.md) can be found to the left of the chess board, one black and one white. Each [piece](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Category%3AChess_pieces) can be picked up and dropped.

### Basketball court

---

- [![The court](../../images/Airport_basketball_court.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_basketball_court.png)
  The court
- [![The basketball trays](../../images/Airport_basketball_trays.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_basketball_trays.png)
  The basketball trays

A miniature basketball court can be found at the opposite end of the airport, in the left corner. It features a basketball hoop with a row of four [![](../../images/Basketball.png)](Basketball.md) [Basketballs](Basketball.md) placed in two small trays for the [Scouts](Scout.md) to pick up. Scouts will automatically dribble the basketballs when walking or running with them. When any object, not just the basketballs, is thrown into the hoop, a sound effect will play and confetti will rain down from the hoop.

### Kiosks

---

- [![Gate Kiosk](../../images/Kiosk_boarding.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AKiosk_boarding.png)
  Gate Kiosk
- [![Invite Kiosk](../../images/Kiosk_invitation.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AKiosk_invitation.png)
  Invite Kiosk
- [![Photobooth Kiosk](../../images/Airport_photo_booth_kiosk.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_photo_booth_kiosk.png)
  Photobooth Kiosk

The Airport houses three interactable **Kiosks**, each having their own purpose:

- The **Gate Kiosk** allows the player to start an expedition on the chosen [Ascent](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ascent) or a [Custom Expedition](Custom%20Expedition.md).
- The **Invite Kiosk** allows the host to invite their Steam friends to the lobby, bringing up Steam's friends list when interacted with. The Invite Kiosk does not appear in offline/solo mode.
- The **Photobooth Kiosk** allows any player to start the [photobooth](#Photobooth) timer.

### Climbing wall

---

[![Airport tower.png](../../images/Airport_tower.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_tower.png)

To the right of the starting elevators is a white indoor climbing wall, allowing Scouts to practice their climbing while waiting for the run to begin. It also sports a rope to climb on as well.

## Trivia

[![](../../images/Airport_schedule.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAirport_schedule.png)

Morse code Easter egg- The rope will fail to spawn after having started a run and then returning back to the airport (via the run ending), causing it to appear only as a tiny static piece of rope attached to the top hook. Without the rope, it is only barely possible to scale the climbing wall from specific angles.
- Aesthetically, the time of the day is eternally noon in the Airport, as the sun is completely stationary. If you glitch out of the Airport and go out of bounds, the stationary sunset will switch back to the sky that changes based on the time of the day in-game.
- The departure times board shows [Morse Code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_code) for the developers' names:- `--.. --- .-. .-. ---` "ZORRO"
  - `-.-. .- . .-.. .- -.` "CAELAN"
  - `.--. . - - . .-.` "PETTER"
  - `. .-. .. -.-` "ERIK"
  - `-. .. -.-. -.-` "NICK"
  - `--. .- .-.. . -.` "GALEN"
  - `.-- .. .-.. .... . .-.. --` "WILHELM"
- An A-posing model of [Scoutmaster Myres](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md) can be found in a normally inaccessible room.
- The Gate Kiosk and Invite Kiosk both show a flight number **1.8.13.6.1.14.13.6**. If the numbers are decoded using the [RFC 4648 Base32 alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Base32#Base_32_Encoding_per_.C2.A76), the flight number spells out **BINGBONG**.
- The Boarding Pass that is displayed when interacting with the Gate Kiosk contains a few Easter Eggs:- On the left side of the Boarding Pass, the official [Peak website's URL](http://peakpeakpeak.com) is encoded as a [PDF417](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PDF417) barcode.
  - The **From** airport code is **ACG**, a reference to [Aggro Crab Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aggro_Crab), one of developers of *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*.
  - The **To** airport code is **LFG**, a refence to [Landfall Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landfall_Games), the other developer of *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*.
- When you are here, your Steam Now Playing will say "In the Airport".
