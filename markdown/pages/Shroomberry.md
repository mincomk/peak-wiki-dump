# Shroomberry

**Shroomberry** is a type of [food](Food.md) found in the [Roots](Roots.md). Consuming a Shroomberry produces a random effect that depends on the [map](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Map) and Shroomberry type.

The Shroomberry has the following variations:

- [![](../../images/Red_Shroomberry.png)](Red%20Shroomberry.md) [Red Shroomberry](Red%20Shroomberry.md)
- [![](../../images/Yellow_Shroomberry.png)](Yellow%20Shroomberry.md) [Yellow Shroomberry](Yellow%20Shroomberry.md)
- [![](../../images/Green_Shroomberry.png)](Green%20Shroomberry.md) [Green Shroomberry](Green%20Shroomberry.md)
- [![](../../images/Blue_Shroomberry.png)](Blue%20Shroomberry.md) [Blue Shroomberry](Blue%20Shroomberry.md)
- [![](../../images/Purple_Shroomberry.png)](Purple%20Shroomberry.md) [Purple Shroomberry](Purple%20Shroomberry.md)

## Effects

There are 10 possible effects a Shroomberry can have, out of which at least 2 good and 2 bad effects are chosen for the current map's five Shroomberries. The effect of one Shroomberry is consistent during that day's map. In addition to an effect, each Shroomberry gives **0–15** [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina), [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **5** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), and removes [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **10** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger). [Cooking](Cooking.md) a Shroomberry removes the base [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **5** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores).

| Random Effect | Variant |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed good | [Red Shroomberry](Red%20Shroomberry.md) [Yellow Shroomberry](Yellow%20Shroomberry.md) |
| Guaranteed bad | [Green Shroomberry](Green%20Shroomberry.md) [Blue Shroomberry](Blue%20Shroomberry.md) |
| Unpredictable | [Purple Shroomberry](Purple%20Shroomberry.md) |

### Good effects

- 4 seconds of **[![Infinite Stamina](../../images/Status_Infinite_Stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) [Infinite Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina)** after a 3 second delay.
- +150% **faster climbing** speed and +50% **movement speed** for 5 seconds (similar to an [![](../../images/Energy_Drink.png)](Energy%20Drink.md) [Energy Drink](Energy%20Drink.md)).
- 15 second **[wind walking](Wind%20walking.md)** after a 3 second delay.
- **[![Invincibility](../../images/Status_Invincibility.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) [Invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility)** for 10 seconds.
- Remove **15** extra [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) (total of **25**), [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **15** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **15** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **15** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) and stop all poison-over-time effects (including [Zombie bite](Zombie.md#Zombie_infection)).

### Bad effects

(Majority trigger after a 3 second delay)

- **[![Numb](../../images/Status_Numb.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Numb) [Numb](Stamina%20bar.md#Numb)** for 60 seconds.
- Loud **particle explosion** that blows away nearby [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts).
- **Blindness** for 60 seconds.
- **[Ragdoll](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragdoll)** state for 8 seconds.
- Inflict **25** extra [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) (total of **30**) - *without delay*.

## Badges

- Eating (or feeding another player) all 5 types of Shroomberry in a single expedition grants the player the [![Advanced Mycology Badge](../../images/Advanced_Mycology_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Mycology_Badge) [Advanced Mycology Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Mycology_Badge).
- Shroomberries count as [berries](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Berries) for the [![Foraging Badge](../../images/Foraging_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Foraging_Badge) [Foraging Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Foraging_Badge).
- Although the name may suggest otherwise, Shroomberries are not considered [mushrooms](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mushrooms) and therefore do not count for the [![Mycology Badge](../../images/Mycology_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mycology_Badge) [Mycology Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mycology_Badge).
