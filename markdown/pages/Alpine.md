# Alpine

The **Alpine** is one of the two possible third [biomes](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*, replacing the [Mesa](Mesa.md) every second day. It follows the [Tropics](Tropics.md) or the [Roots](Roots.md) and precedes the [Caldera](Caldera.md) or [Gloom](Gloom.md). It is a snowy, hilly area with a cliffside full of caves.

Reaching the top and lighting the [campfire](Campfire.md) dispels the fog to the next biome and grants all living [Scouts](Scout.md) the [![Alpinist Badge](../../images/Alpinist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Alpinist_Badge) [Alpinist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Alpinist_Badge) if they haven't already obtained it. Additionally, players who have never had more than [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **20** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) at any time during their climb through the Alpine receive the [![Bundled Up Badge](../../images/Bundled_Up_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bundled_Up_Badge) [Bundled Up Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Bundled_Up_Badge).

## Features

### Trees

---

#### Pine Trees

[![Alpine tree 1.jpg](../../images/Alpine_tree_1.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAlpine_tree_1.jpg)

**Pine Trees** can be found throughout the Alpine. Some of these trees are dead and lack foliage. Although some variants visually resemble the [Redwood Trees](Roots.md#Redwood_Trees) found in the [Roots](Roots.md), players cannot stand on any part of these trees' canopies.

#### Pine Shrubs

**Pine Shrubs** are small dome shaped bushes that can be stood on.

#### Winterberry Trees

[![Alpine tree 2.jpg](../../images/Alpine_tree_2.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAlpine_tree_2.jpg)

**Winterberry Trees** adorn the Alpine with their mycelium-like foliage. These trees have branches thick enough to be stood upon. Most of these trees are fruitless, but it is not uncommon to find trees with multiple [![](../../images/Orange_Winterberry.png)](Orange%20Winterberry.md) [Orange Winterberries](Orange%20Winterberry.md) hanging off their branches. Extremely rarely, a player might find a tree that bears [![](../../images/Yellow_Winterberry.png)](Yellow%20Winterberry.md) [Yellow Winterberries](Yellow%20Winterberry.md).

### Caves

---

- [![Alpine caves.jpg](../../images/Alpine_caves.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAlpine_caves.jpg)
- [![Alpine caves2.jpg](../../images/Alpine_caves2.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAlpine_caves2.jpg)

The Alpine's cliffside can house large, open **Caves**. Trees with [Winterberries](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Winterberry) on them can often be found growing in these caves.

### Hot Spring

---

[![Alpine hot spring.jpg](../../images/Alpine_hot_spring.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AAlpine_hot_spring.jpg)

A **Hot Spring** can be found higher up the Alpine's cliffside. It is a small pool of steaming water housing three [capybaras](Capybara.md). Standing in the water will remove [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) with time and protect you from [blizzards](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Blizzards). One to three of the capybaras will have a [![](../../images/Yellow_Winterberry.png)](Yellow%20Winterberry.md) [Yellow Winterberry](Yellow%20Winterberry.md) resting on their heads.

The Hot Spring is the Alpine's equivalent to the [Oasis](Mesa.md#Oasis) in the [Mesa](Mesa.md).

### Rusty Pitons

---

[![](../../images/Rusty_piton_alpine.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ARusty_piton_alpine.png)

A rusty [Piton](Piton.md)

**[Rusty Pitons](Piton.md#Rusty_Pitons)** can be found embedded in the walls of the Alpine's caves. [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) can grab onto the handle of a Rusty Piton to recover stamina identical to a normal [![](../../images/Piton.png)](Piton.md) [Piton](Piton.md), but will break after being held onto for **6 seconds**.

The Rusty Pitons are the Alpine's equivalent to the [Pickaxes](Mesa.md#Pickaxes) found in the [Mesa](Mesa.md).

## Tips & Tricks

- [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) can huddle up together to increase the speed at which [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) fades. When in a group, always find cover that can house everyone in the event of a [blizzard](Blizzard.md).
- If possible, bringing [![](../../images/Scorchberry.png)](Scorchberry.md) [Scorchberries](Scorchberry.md) from the [Tropics](Tropics.md) can help with fighting the Blizzard's cold, especially alone, as the [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) gained from them will cancel out the [cold](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cold).
- A [geyser's](Geyser.md) imminent eruption can be foretold by the sound of steam building up and a shaking screen. Once this starts to happen, you have a short amount of time to get away from the [geyser](Geyser.md).

## Trivia

- This is the biome that [Scoutmaster Myres](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md) was left behind in, after he passed out from the effects of a [![](../../images/Napberry.png)](Napberry.md) [Napberry](Napberry.md).
- When you are in this biome, your Steam Now Playing will say "Weathering the Alpine".
