The **Anti-Rope Cannon** is a [deployable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Deployable) [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. Using an Anti-Rope Cannon attaches a **7.25 meter** anti-rope (see [![](../../images/Anti-Rope_Spool.png)](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md) [Anti-Rope Spool](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md)) to a surface up to **48 meters** away, embedding one end of it into the surface via a hook. It is the anti version of the [![](../../images/Rope_Cannon.png)](Rope%20Cannon.md) [Rope Cannon](Rope%20Cannon.md). The Anti-Rope Cannon is a [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) and as such can only be found in [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md) and inside [Ancient Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) an Anti-Rope Cannon immediately incinerates it, making it unusable.

## Physics

The Anti-Rope Cannon has zero weight and will float mid-air when dropped. It will retain its momentum, however, so care still has to be taken by players not to lose it.

The physics of ropes deployed by the Anti-Rope Cannon are identical to the [![](../../images/Rope_Cannon.png)](Rope%20Cannon.md) [Rope Cannon](Rope%20Cannon.md), with the lone exception being that anti-ropes unspool upwards. The same tips present in said article apply, but vertically flipped.
