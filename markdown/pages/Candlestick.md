The **Candlestick** is an [item](Items.md) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It can only be found in luggage in the [Gloom](Gloom.md) or [The Citadel](The%20Citadel.md).

While lit, all players within **7 meters** of the candle are immune to the [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) inflicted by the [Sleepy Gloom](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sleepy_Gloom), and the visuals of the Gloom are dispelled within **4 meters**. The Candlestick can be lit for **30 seconds** before running out of usage.

A Candlestick can be used to burn [The Ghost](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Ghost), granting the player the [![Exorcist Badge](../../images/Exorcist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge) [Exorcist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Exorcist_Badge).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Candlestick has no effect.
