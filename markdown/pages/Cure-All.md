The **Cure-All** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It can be found in [Ancient Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage) or obtained from an [Ancient Statue](Ancient%20Statue.md). It has **3 uses**, and when drunk, it removes a wide array of status effects from the [Stamina bar](Stamina%20bar.md). Drinking the Cure-All also removes poison over time effects from the player but it does not remove drowsy over time effects from the player.

Dropping or [cooking](Cooking.md) the Cure-All causes it to shatter, removing up to [![Hunger](../../images/Status_Hunger.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger) **10** [Hunger](Stamina%20bar.md#Hunger), [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **20** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat), [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **20** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **20** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison), [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **20** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy), [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **20** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), [![Thorns](../../images/Status_Thorns.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns) **20** [Thorns](Stamina%20bar.md#Thorns), and [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) **5** [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) from players up to **4.8 meters** away, depending on their distance. Dropped or cooked Cure-All does not prevent poison over time effects that have not elapsed from applying additional poison to the player. Throwing a Cure-All directly at a player however will bonk them, causing them to enter a [ragdoll](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragdoll) state.

## Tips

- Because the shatter effect is always the same, it's often most efficient to throw and shatter the cure-all after using it twice, allowing multiple scouts to be healed with the final use. This does not apply in a solo run, as the shatter heals less than simply drinking it.
