# Zombie

Zombie
[![Zombie run.jpg](../../images/Zombie_run.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AZombie_run.jpg)

Type
[Enemy](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemy)

**Zombies** are a hostile [NPC](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/NPC) encountered in the [Roots](Roots.md) when playing on [Peak ascent](Ascents.md#Peak) or higher. They are corpses of [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts), reanimated by parasitic fungi.

## Spawning and behavior

Zombies spawn asleep, lying on the ground of the biome. If a player gets within the line of sight of a Zombie, it will wake up and begin to chase after them. Every **10 seconds**, the Zombie will switch its target to the closest player. Zombies have infinite stamina and can climb any surfaces to reach their target players.

Once close enough to the targeted player, the Zombie will lunge at them, [ragdolling](Scout.md#Ragdoll_State) in the process. Zombies do not lunge at players while jumping on [bouncy mushrooms](Bounce%20Fungus.md). If the Zombie makes contact with the target while lunging, it will bite them, inflicting [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **10** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) and [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **10** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores), while also giving them the zombie infection. If the player does not have any Spores and is [invincible](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) when bitten, the player will not be infected. If the target becomes unconscious, the Zombie will select a new one.

After chasing the target for **~2 minutes**[[1]](#cite_note-1), or after the [campfire](Campfire.md) at the top of the biome is lit, the Zombie will die the next time it [ragdolls](Scout.md#Ragdoll_State), leaving a [skeleton](Skeleton.md) behind.

## Zombie infection

**Zombie infection** is the result of a Zombie bite. An infected player will gain [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **0.5** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) every second (2.5 every 5 seconds) until cured. The infection can be cured in the following ways:

- Using any [item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Item) that removes [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores).
- Maintaining [Invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) for a continuous 45 seconds to allow natural spore decay.
- Surviving 60 seconds to let the infection run its course. Additional bites while infected do not reset this timer.
- Reducing the player's spores to zero. This can be achieved by various means:- A player resurrected by a [![](../../images/Checkpoint_Flag.png)](Checkpoint%20Flag.md) [Checkpoint Flag](Checkpoint%20Flag.md) will be cured if they had [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **0** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) when the flag was placed.
  - A lucky [![](../../images/Pandora%27s_Lunchbox.png)](Pandora%27s%20Lunchbox.md) [Pandora's Lunchbox](Pandora%27s%20Lunchbox.md) roll.
  - Thrown and cooked [![](../../images/Antidote.png)](Antidote.md) [Antidote](Antidote.md) or [![](../../images/Cure-All.png)](Cure-All.md) [Cure-All](Cure-All.md) only stop the infection when the player has [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **0** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores).
  - Using [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Tenacity.png)](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) [Scout's Tenacity](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) to remove all spores.
  - Using a [![](../../images/The_Book_of_Bones.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_Bones) [Book of Bones](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_Bones) to transform into a skeleton will clear all [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) immediately.

Dying with at least [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) **50** [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) makes the player's corpse into a new Zombie.

An infected player will receive the [![Undead Encounter Badge](../../images/Undead_Encounter_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead_Encounter_Badge) [Undead Encounter Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead_Encounter_Badge) once all of their [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) are removed.

## Interactions

Zombies interact with their environment similarly to Scouts in certain aspects.

- Certain thrown items can bonk a Zombie, causing them to enter a [ragdoll](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ragdoll) state. These include:- [![](../../images/Coconut.png)](Coconut.md) [Coconut](Coconut.md)
  - [![](../../images/Flying_Disc.png)](Flying%20Disc.md) [Flying Disc](Flying%20Disc.md)
  - [![](../../images/Cure-All.png)](Cure-All.md) [Cure-All](Cure-All.md)
  - [![](../../images/Antidote.png)](Antidote.md) [Antidote](Antidote.md)
- The [![](../../images/Rescue_Claw.png)](Rescue%20Claw.md) [Rescue Claw](Rescue%20Claw.md) can be used to pull Zombies.
- Zombies are immune to most status effects but are affected by [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy). If sufficient Drowsy is inflicted on a Zombie, it will be knocked out and die after ~60 seconds. Drowsy can be applied using the following methods:- Shooting a Zombie with a [![](../../images/Blowgun.png)](Blowgun.md) [Blowgun](Blowgun.md). This will knock out a Zombie long enough for it to die.
  - A [![](../../images/Mandrake.png)](Mandrake.md) [Mandrake](Mandrake.md) near a player will scream and apply Drowsy to a Zombie if it is close enough and the player does not have their sound effects volume set to 0 . The Zombie may regain consciousness after some time if the Mandrake stops screaming.
- Zombies are affected by certain environmental hazards:- Zombies can trigger [Explosive Bombshrooms](Roots.md#Explosive_Bombshroom) and are affected by their knockback.
  - Zombies can trigger [Spore Bombshrooms](Roots.md#Spore_Bombshroom).
  - Zombies will bounce on [Giant Bounce Fungi](Roots.md#Giant_Bounce_Fungi) and [Bounce Fungus](Bounce%20Fungus.md).
  - Zombies will cause [Spiders](Spider.md) to drop down but will not be grabbed.
  - [Wind](Roots.md#Wind) will push Zombies.
  - [Beetles](Roots.md#Beetle) will not target Zombies.
  - [Waterfalls](Roots.md#Waterfalls) will knock over Zombies that touch them.
- Zombies are affected by knockback caused by explosions.
- Zombies in a [![](../../images/Scout_Cannon.png)](Scout%20Cannon.md) [Scout Cannon](Scout%20Cannon.md) can be launched.
- A Scout with a Zombie above them can crouch jump to hurl the Zombie into the air.
- Zombies do not [ragdoll](Scout.md#Ragdoll_State) when falling great heights.

## Tips

- Zombies lunge in a straight line, so it's not too hard to sidestep the lunge and send the Zombie flying down the mountain
- If a player sees a Zombie before it sees them, it's generally a good idea to avoid waking the Zombie up.
- Zombies will wait a few seconds after waking up or getting up from being [ragdolled](Scout.md#Ragdoll_State) before lunging again, so make distance before attempting to dodge its lunge. When the Zombie is about to lunge, it will sprint toward a Scout instead of walking.
- Zombies appear as anthropomorphic [![](../../images/Chubby_Shroom.png)](Chubby%20Shroom.md) [Chubby Shrooms](Chubby%20Shroom.md) while using [Zombie Phobia Mode](Zombie%20Phobia%20Mode.md). This mode also disables their grunting sound effect.

## Trivia

- The Zombies appear to reference the parasitic behavior of various [cordyceps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cordyceps) mushrooms found in nature.
- If a player's body is reanimated through zombie infection, the newly created Zombie retains all cosmetics of the host, with the exception of the eyes.- The eyes and spores of Zombies are compatible with living Scouts, but it is impossible to obtain these cosmetics without using mods.
- The original Zombie shares a striking resemblance to the [Looker](Looker.md) NPC.
- In the unlikely event of a Zombie ending up in the [Gloom](Gloom.md), it will not accumulate Drowsy from traversing the fog cloud, despite being susceptible to other sources of Drowsy.
- It is possible for [Arrows](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Arrows) to get stuck in a Zombie's body just like living Scouts, although the chances of a Zombie finding its way into [the Citadel](The%20Citadel.md) to get hit by an Arrow Trap are extremely low. Should this happen, the arrows will not hinder the Zombie.

## Patch history

![Peak Badge.png](../../images/Peak_Badge.png) **[1.40.a](1.40.a.md) (2025-11-05):** Introduced.
![Peak Badge.png](../../images/Peak_Badge.png) **[1.42.a](1.42.a.md) (2025-11-05):**

- Removed zombies entirely from Tenderfoot difficulty.
- Significantly reduced the number of zombie spawn points on the map.
- Slightly reduced zombie walk and sprint speed.
- Increased the amount of time a zombie will take to recover after a lunge.
- Reduced the radius at which a sleeping zombie will wake up and begin chasing the player.
- Reduced the distance at which a zombie will go from a walk to a sprint.
- Zombies now attack skeletonised players.

![Peak Badge.png](../../images/Peak_Badge.png) **[1.43.a](1.43.a.md) (2025-11-07):**

- Zombies will now recalculate their aggro target and begin chasing the closest player every ~10 seconds.
- Fixed an issue where a player's zombie could bite you on the first frame they turned into a zombie.

![Peak Badge.png](../../images/Peak_Badge.png) **[1.44.a](1.44.a.md) (2025-11-13):**

- The death timer now still ticks down even when carried by another scout if you have enough spores to become a zombie. Good luck...
- Fixed an issue where Zombie footsteps would play from the wrong position.
- Fixed an issue where a spawn point could spawn a zombie more than once if an old one died, resulting in some comedic infinite spawns if the spawned zombie ragdolled off a cliff.
- [Scout Cannons](Scout%20Cannon.md) can launch zombies now.

![Peak Badge.png](../../images/Peak_Badge.png) **[1.45.a](1.45.a.md) (2025-11-17):** Fixed an issue where players could still become zombies in [Tenderfoot](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Tenderfoot), despite zombies not spawning in the world.
