The **Dynamite** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in [luggage](Luggage.md) or on the ground of the [Mesa](Mesa.md), or rarely in other biomes inside [Clown Luggages](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Clown_Luggage). If a scout is within **8 meters** of the dynamite (or it is found inside a luggage) the fuse will be automatically lit, exploding after 5 seconds. The explosion can cause up to [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **27.5** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) to players within **9.6 meters**, and an additional [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **25** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) if held.

It is possible to pick up and throw Dynamite, should there be no safe place to retreat from it or too many clueless [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) nearby.

Attempting to [cook](Cooking.md) Dynamite will cause it to immediately detonate. Dynamite explosions cook other items in close proximity, which can cause chain reactions if those items happen to be more Dynamite.

The Dynamite can also be used as a fuel for [Jetpacks](Jetpack.md) making it useful and non-dangerous if timed right

The label text "FIVE BIG BOOMS" may be a reference to [this meme.](https://youtu.be/wHryuEcgSXY)
