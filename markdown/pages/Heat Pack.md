The **Heat Pack** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. Using a Heat Pack removes [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) **6** [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) per second for the next 60 seconds. Heat packs only spawn in [luggages](Luggage.md) in the [Alpine](Alpine.md).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Heat Pack causes it to explode, dealing up to [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) **20** [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) to players within **4.8 meters**, depending on their distance.
