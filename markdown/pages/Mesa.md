# Mesa

Mesa
[![Mesa Location.png](../../images/Mesa_Location.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_Location.png)
Details
Type
[Biome](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome)
Region
**3**/6

The **Mesa** is one of the two possible third [biomes](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*, replacing the [Alpine](Alpine.md) every two days. It follows the [Tropics](Tropics.md) or the [Roots](Roots.md) and precedes the [Caldera](Caldera.md) or the [Gloom](Gloom.md). It is a dry, hot desert separated by a large canyon.

The main hazard of the Mesa is its unique blistering [sun](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun) which forces players to stay in the shade.

Reaching the top and lighting the [campfire](Campfire.md) dispels the fog to the next biome and grants all living [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) the [![Nomad Badge](../../images/Nomad_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Nomad_Badge) [Nomad Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Nomad_Badge) if they have not already obtained it.

## Areas & Features

### Plateau

---

[![Mesa 3.jpg](../../images/Mesa_3.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_3.jpg)

The **Plateau** is the first area of the Mesa, preceding the [Canyon](#Canyon). Lots of [Big Cacti](#Cacti) can be found here, along with various rock formations, some of which occasionally form arches.

The Plateau houses some of the Mesa's exclusive [hazards](#Hazards), such as [tornadoes](Tornado.md) and [antlion traps](Antlion.md). [Tumbleweeds](Tumbleweed.md) can also be seen rolling around the Plateau, knocking unaware [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) over.

The Plateau is largely open to the Mesa's [sun](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun), so players have to plan their route carefully and use the rock formations to stay in the shade.

#### Oasis

[![Mesa oasis 2.jpg](../../images/Mesa_oasis_2.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_oasis_2.jpg)

An **Oasis** can be found on the Plateau. It is comprised of a small pool of cold water surrounded by palm trees with [![](../../images/Coconut.png)](Coconut.md) [coconuts](Coconut.md). The pool houses two [capybaras](Capybara.md), one of which is guaranteed to hold a [![](../../images/Yellow_Winterberry.png)](Yellow%20Winterberry.md) [Yellow Winterberry](Yellow%20Winterberry.md) on its head while the other wears a cowboy hat. Standing in the pool removes any accumulated [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) over a short period of time.

The Oasis is the Mesa's equivalent to the [Hot Spring](Alpine.md#Hot_Spring) in the [Alpine](Alpine.md).

#### Tomb entrance

Main article: [Tomb](Tomb.md)
[![Tomb hole.jpg](../../images/Tomb_hole.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ATomb_hole.jpg)

An entrance to the [Tomb](Tomb.md) can be found on the Plateau. Whether or not the entrance is open is dependent on the current map rotation, though the exit may be randomly blocked by map generation. Refer to [this guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3553972295) for more details.

### Canyon

---

[![Mesa canyon 1.jpg](../../images/Mesa_canyon_1.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_canyon_1.jpg)

The **Canyon** is the second area of the Mesa which separates the Plateau and the [Caves](#Caves). Players need to climb down and then back up again to pass the Canyon. Alternatively, they may use a [![](../../images/Scout_Cannon.png)](Scout%20Cannon.md) [Scout Cannon](Scout%20Cannon.md) to fly past the area, which grants the [![Daredevil Badge](../../images/Daredevil_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Daredevil_Badge) [Daredevil Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Daredevil_Badge).

The deeper parts of the Canyon are hidden from the [sun](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun), although players should be careful around the surface, as the sun can still reach them there. The [![](../../images/Warp_Fungus.png)](Warp%20Fungus.md) [Warp Fungus](Warp%20Fungus.md) can be used to teleport across the **Canyon.**

### Caves

- [![The vertical portion of Mesa containing the Caves](../../images/Mesa_wall.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_wall.png)
  The vertical portion of Mesa containing the Caves
- [![The Caves up-close](../../images/Mesa_caves_1.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMesa_caves_1.jpg)
  The Caves up-close

The **Caves** are the final area of the Mesa. Players will have to climb all the way up to reach the [campfire](Campfire.md). The inner caves are well-hidden from the [sun](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun), even though occasionally players may have to climb the outer parts of the Caves which are exposed to the sun.

#### Mine Shafts

[![Mineshaft 2.jpg](../../images/Mineshaft_2.jpg)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3AMineshaft_2.jpg)

Maze-like **Mine Shafts** can occasionally be found in the caves. They usually hold at least 1 [regular luggage](Luggage.md#Regular_Luggage) crate, but will more often than not also contain several pieces of [![](../../images/Dynamite.png)](Dynamite.md) [Dynamite](Dynamite.md), which can often catch players off guard.

#### Pickaxes

[![Pickaxe.png](../../images/Pickaxe.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3APickaxe.png)

Old **[Pickaxes](Piton.md#Pickaxes)** can spawn naturally in the cliffs of the Caves. They perform identically to [Rusty Pitons](Piton.md), breaking after **6 seconds** of being held on to.

**Mirages** happen exclusively in the Mesa. Players may see mirages of [Luggage](Luggage.md) or even entire [oases](#Oasis). They appear like normal objects from far away, but will begin to fade out as the player approaches them.

## Tips & Tricks

- Mirages can be determined by using the ping system. If the ping hand lands on the [Luggage](Luggage.md) or [Oasis](#Oasis) tree normally, then it is real, but if it hits the ground behind it instead, it's a mirage. This method can be used to avoid going after fake luggage and wasting time.
- If possible, wait until night time to traverse the Mesa's first half. The lack of burning sun will make the journey to the [Canyon](#Canyon) much smoother. This might not be applicable on [Ascent 5](Ascents.md) and higher, due to the night's [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) nature.
- If it is *not* ideal to wait until night to cross the Plateau, players can time their movements with the shadows, which shift rather quickly as the sun moves across the sky, in order to avoid gaining [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat).
- Use the Canyon's depth to your advantage during the day, as it will protect you from the sun until you reach the cliffside.
- Both [![](../../images/Scorpion.png)](Scorpion.md) [Scorpions](Scorpion.md) and [![](../../images/Dynamite.png)](Dynamite.md) [Dynamite](Dynamite.md) can be quickly picked up and tossed away with the right timing. If you see a Scorpion or Dynamite in your path, grab it and toss it as far as you can. You can charge the throw meter almost all the way up before the Scorpion even begins to charge its attack.
- You can stop the sun from burning you by holding an item and pointing it directly at the sun. The game only checks for a line of sight between the sun and your [Scout](Scout.md)'s center, so breaking it will prevent the sun from causing [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat).
- [Tornadoes](Tornado.md) can be difficult to keep track of; they move quickly and unpredictably and can approach from any direction, including from behind rock formations. A visual swirling-up of sand serves to alert players when a tornado is extremely nearby. In these situations, it is often too late to run away from the tornado, but crouching is sometimes enough to avoid being caught up and thrown. Grabbing onto a wall rarely helps.

## Trivia

- The Mesa is the first [biome](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome) to be added after the game's initial launch.
- The Mesa is the first biome that can potentially replace another biome in an [island](Island.md) rotation.
- When you are in this biome, your Steam Now Playing will say "Surviving the Mesa".
