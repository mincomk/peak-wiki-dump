The **First Aid Kit** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It is found in [luggage](Luggage.md).

Using the First Aid Kit removes any [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury), [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) (including any remaining poison over time effects), or [![Spores](../../images/Status_Spores.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) [Spores](Stamina%20bar.md#Spores) a [Scout](Scout.md) may have.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a First Aid Kit has no effect, even when incinerated.
