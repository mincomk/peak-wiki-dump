The **Cursed Skull** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. Unlike other mystical items, it can only spawn in [Ancient Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage) and is not obtainable through [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md). When used, the player who activates the Cursed Skull will die immediately, and all other players within **1440 meters** will gain [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **100** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) and be cleared of all [afflictions](Stamina%20bar.md) except [![Curse](../../images/Status_Curse.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) [Curse](Stamina%20bar.md#Curse) (and potentially [Petrify](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Petrify)).

Attempting to [cook](Cooking.md) a Cursed Skull immediately activates it and creates a [dynamite](Dynamite.md) explosion, destroying the item.

- If the Cursed Skull is cooked by throwing it into Lava, leaving it on heated ground or by using explosives, the Cursed Skull will activate without killing any scouts.
- If lava or heated ground causes the Cursed Skull in a held [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md) to be cooked, the player will not die but will take [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) damage and knockback from the explosion.

If a player has placed a [Checkpoint Flag](Checkpoint%20Flag.md) and uses the Cursed Skull, they will gain [![Bonus stamina](../../images/Status_Bonus_stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) **50** [Bonus stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Bonus_stamina) but will otherwise follow normal Checkpoint Flag respawning rules.

The Cursed Skull cannot spawn when playing solo.
