# Flytrap

**Flytraps** are hostile [NPCs](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/NPC) found in the [Gloom](Gloom.md). They can trap unaware [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) in their jaws, inflicting [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) until the Scout escapes.

## Mechanics

When a [Scout](Scout.md) walks on top of a Flytrap, it will close its jaws and trap the Scout who touched it. The trapped Scout is immediately inflicted with [![Flytrap](../../images/Status_Flytrap.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Flytrap) **30** [Flytrap](Stamina%20bar.md#Flytrap). The trapped Scout can struggle out of the jaws by pressing jump (![Keyboard space.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_space.png/25px-Keyboard_space.png?9ad9c4)), removing [![Flytrap](../../images/Status_Flytrap.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Flytrap) **2.5** [Flytrap](Stamina%20bar.md#Flytrap) per press of the key. Note that struggling requires [stamina](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina) and stamina is still regained while trapped. A trapped Scout cannot use any items or access their [backpack](Backpack.md).

Once the Flytrap has trapped its victim, it will begin dealing [![Poison](../../images/Status_Poison.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) **5** [Poison](Stamina%20bar.md#Poison) to them every second.

Other [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) can also free a trapped Scout by grabbing them with **Helping Hand**.

## Interactions

- A player with [invincibility](Stamina%20bar.md#Invincibility) will be immediately released after being trapped by a flytrap. This will still briefly knock the player down.
- A player that is trapped can be freed from afar by shooting them with a [![](../../images/Blowgun.png)](Blowgun.md) [Blowgun](Blowgun.md).
- A player can also be freed by being fed a [![](../../images/Napberry.png)](Napberry.md) [Napberry](Napberry.md). If the trapped player is close enough to be fed, it is probably best to instead use **Helping Hand**.
