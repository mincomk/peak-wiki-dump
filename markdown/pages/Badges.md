# Badges

**Badges**, also known as **Accolades** and **Achievements**, are granted to players of *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* upon completing certain actions. Once obtained, they are displayed on the [Scout](Scout.md)'s equipped [sash](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sash). Every badge has an associated reward [cosmetic](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cosmetic) item. There are a total of **64** badges.

A full list of badges can be viewed in-game on the Accolades screen accessible via the pause menu. Hovering over a badge shows its name and description. Badges which are not yet unlocked are greyed out, with their names replaced with question marks. The player's current [sash](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sash) and successful expeditions count are also shown.

All badges, except [![Undead Encounter Badge](../../images/Undead_Encounter_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead_Encounter_Badge) [Undead Encounter Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Undead_Encounter_Badge) and [![Mentorship Badge](../../images/Mentorship_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mentorship_Badge) [Mentorship Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mentorship_Badge), can be earned under all difficulties, including [Tenderfoot](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Tenderfoot). Badges cannot be earned on [Custom Expeditions](Custom%20Expedition.md).

## Tips And Tricks

- For the [![24 Karat Badge](../../images/24_Karat_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/24_Karat_Badge) [24 Karat Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/24_Karat_Badge), focus on prioritizing items that make traversal of the mountain easier, like the [![](../../images/Chain_Launcher.png)](Chain%20Launcher.md) [Chain Launcher](Chain%20Launcher.md), [![](../../images/Rope_Cannon.png)](Rope%20Cannon.md) [Rope Cannon](Rope%20Cannon.md), or [![](../../images/Anti-Rope_Spool.png)](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md) [Anti-Rope Spool](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md). This is because regular climbs will be much harder with the [![](../../images/Ancient_Idol.png)](Ancient%20Idol.md) [Ancient Idol](Ancient%20Idol.md)'s added weight, so items like these help in making things easier. The biome after the [MESA](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/MESA) should be much easier, as the [![](../../images/Ancient_Idol.png)](Ancient%20Idol.md) [Ancient Idol](Ancient%20Idol.md) protects against the [CALDERA](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/CALDERA)'s lava and the [GLOOM](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/GLOOM)'s gloom. You are also going to want [![](../../images/Balloon.png)](Balloon.md) [Balloons](Balloon.md) and [![](../../images/Balloon_Bunch.png)](Balloon%20Bunch.md) [Balloon Bunches](Balloon%20Bunch.md), as these items decrease the weight that the [![](../../images/Ancient_Idol.png)](Ancient%20Idol.md) [Ancient Idol](Ancient%20Idol.md) adds.
- For the [![Advanced Mycology Badge](../../images/Advanced_Mycology_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Mycology_Badge) [Advanced Mycology Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Advanced_Mycology_Badge), take your time looking through the [ROOTS](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/ROOTS) for every single shroomberry. Look at your surroundings as much as you can before you progress upwards to search for shroomberries.
- For the [![Aeronautics Badge](../../images/Aeronautics_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Aeronautics_Badge) [Aeronautics Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Aeronautics_Badge), search every luggage you see to find as many [![](../../images/Balloon.png)](Balloon.md) [Balloons](Balloon.md) as possible.
- For the [![Animal Serenading Badge](../../images/Animal_Serenading_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge) [Animal Serenading Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Animal_Serenading_Badge), it is advised to either memorize the location of the [Capybaras](Capybara.md) from a previous run, or wait until the [MESA](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/MESA) is the third biome of the mountain, as the [Capybaras](Capybara.md) will be much easier to find. The [![](../../images/Bugle.png)](Bugle.md) [Bugle](Bugle.md) should be kept in the [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md) or [![](../../images/Fanny_Pack.png)](Fanny%20Pack.md) [Fanny Pack](Fanny%20Pack.md) if you have them, since you only need it until you find the [Capybaras](Capybara.md), which leaves room for more valuable items in the hotbar.
- For the [![Applied Esoterica Badge](../../images/Applied_Esoterica_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Applied_Esoterica_Badge) [Applied Esoterica Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Applied_Esoterica_Badge), even though it may seem counter-intuitive, prioritize keeping all of your teammates alive over anything else. This is so you can get [mystical items](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) from the [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md), and more specifically, [![](../../images/The_Book_of_Bones.png)](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md) [The Book of Bones](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md). Once you get [![](../../images/The_Book_of_Bones.png)](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md) [The Book of Bones](The%20Book%20of%20Bones.md), it should be fairly simple to get a teammate killed via any map hazards or enemies.
- For the [![Arborist Badge](../../images/Arborist_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Arborist_Badge) [Arborist Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Arborist_Badge), it is easier to climb most of the mountain and search for the top of a big tree from there, rather than climbing the tree from the bottom or middle. Items like the [![](../../images/Anti-Rope_Spool.png)](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md) [Anti-Rope Spool](Anti-Rope%20Spool.md) or the [![](../../images/Rope_Cannon.png)](Rope%20Cannon.md) [Rope Cannon](Rope%20Cannon.md) will be helpful if you plan on climbing the tree to reach the top.
- For the [![Archery Badge](../../images/Archery_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Archery_Badge) [Archery Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Archery_Badge), items like [![](../../images/Fortified_Milk.png)](Fortified%20Milk.md) [Fortified Milk](Fortified%20Milk.md), [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Tenacity.png)](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) [Scout's Tenacity](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md), and the [![](../../images/Ancient_Idol.png)](Ancient%20Idol.md) [Ancient Idol](Ancient%20Idol.md) will help when being hit by the arrows or removing them from yourself, as it prevents you from taking any [Arrows](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Arrows) or [Injury](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Injury). Healing items like the [![](../../images/Bandages.png)](Bandages.md) [Bandages](Bandages.md) or [![](../../images/First_Aid_Kit.png)](First%20Aid%20Kit.md) [First Aid Kit](First%20Aid%20Kit.md) will also be helpful, as they will remove any [Injury](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Injury) that you take from removing arrows.
- For the [![Astronomy Badge](../../images/Astronomy_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Astronomy_Badge) [Astronomy Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Astronomy_Badge), the [![](../../images/Binoculars.png)](Binoculars.md) [Binoculars](Binoculars.md) should be kept in the [![](../../images/Backpack.png)](Backpack.md) [Backpack](Backpack.md) or [![](../../images/Fanny_Pack.png)](Fanny%20Pack.md) [Fanny Pack](Fanny%20Pack.md) if you have them, since you only need it until you get to the [MESA](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/MESA), which leaves room for more valuable items in the hotbar.
- For the [![Last Resort Badge](../../images/Last_Resort_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Last_Resort_Badge) [Last Resort Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Last_Resort_Badge), 75 status of any kind will work. The [![](../../images/Blowgun.png)](Blowgun.md) [Blowgun](Blowgun.md) can be used to inflict more than enough [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) to earn the badge as soon as you wake up.
- For the [![Megaentomology Badge](../../images/Megaentomology_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Megaentomology_Badge) [Megaentomology Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Megaentomology_Badge), it's recommended to bring at least a [![](../../images/Fortified_Milk.png)](Fortified%20Milk.md) [Fortified Milk](Fortified%20Milk.md) or a [![](../../images/Scout%27s_Tenacity.png)](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) [Scout's Tenacity](Scout%27s%20Tenacity.md) to protect yourself from the Antlion's damage, as you'll need almost all of your [stamina](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina) to escape the mound.- If you have an [![](../../images/Anti-Rope_Cannon.png)](Anti-Rope%20Cannon.md) [Anti-Rope Cannon](Anti-Rope%20Cannon.md), you can aim it at the center of the mound to escape easily. After the Antlion bites you, grab the anti-rope, climb it, and jump out of the mound.
  - The [![](../../images/Anti-Zooka.png)](Anti-Zooka.md) [Anti-Zooka](Anti-Zooka.md) is also useful if you aim it at the center of the mound: it lets you regenerate stamina, makes the Antlion push you outward with more force, and in some cases, can prevent further bites.
  - If you have a [![](../../images/Jetpack.png)](Jetpack.md) [Jetpack](Jetpack.md), you can jump directly into the center of the mound, let the Antlion push you upward, and then use the Jetpack to fly out of it.
