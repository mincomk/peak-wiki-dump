The **Sunscreen** is a [consumable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Consumable) item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in [luggage](Luggage.md) in the [Mesa](Mesa.md). It can be used to create a small cloud spanning **4.8 meters** that will apply a sunscreen effect to any players passing through it, offering **90 seconds** of protection from the sun's [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) damage in the [Mesa](Mesa.md). Sunscreen will appear as a white sheen on the [Scout](Scout.md)'s skin and a white cloudy texture around the edges of the screen. The sunscreen cloud will dissipate after **4 seconds**.

[Cooking](Cooking.md) a Sunscreen causes it to explode, creating a cloud **9.6 meters** across that gives any scouts within the sunscreen effect for the normal **90 seconds**. This cloud also dissipates after **4 seconds**.
