The **Scout's Ambition** is a [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. As an [amulet](Amulets.md), it can be obtained from one of the [Amulet Statues](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Amulet_Statues) found across the mountain. Upon use, a yellow bubble is formed for **8 seconds** around the user with a range of **9.6 meters**, granting [![Infinite Stamina](../../images/Status_Infinite_Stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) [Infinite Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) to the user and all Scouts within the bubble. The user gains [![Petrify](../../images/Status_Petrify.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) **30** [Petrify](Stamina%20bar.md#Petrify) upon use.

Scout's Ambition cannot be [cooked](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cooked).

## Interpretation

Each [amulet](Amulets.md) and its respective ability embodies a particular trait that [Scoutmaster Myres](Scoutmaster%20Myres.md)' troop had. Ambition is defined as the *"strong desire to achieve a particular end."* In this context, the objective would be to reach the [Peak](Peak%20%28biome%29.md), and to have [![Infinite Stamina](../../images/Status_Infinite_Stamina.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) [Infinite Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md#Infinite_Stamina) is to show ambition to do so. From a philosophical perspective, one could imagine each [amulet](Amulets.md) to greatly intensify the already present traits within the [player character](Scout.md) to allow to achieve otherwise impossible feats rather than to directly have supernatural abilities.
