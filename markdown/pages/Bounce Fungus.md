# Bounce Fungus

Bounce Fungus
[![Bounce Fungus.png](../../images/Bounce_Fungus.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ABounce_Fungus.png)
[Item type](Items.md)- [Deployable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Deployable)
Biome- [Roots](Roots.md)
Weight
[![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) **2.5**

The **Bounce Fungus** is an item in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* and can be found on steep surfaces in the [Roots](Roots.md). It can be thrown or dropped to create a bouncy platform at the point of impact with terrain, an item, player, or other object with valid collision. Thrown items and falling players can also cause a Bounce Fungus to become a platform.

Bouncing on a Bounce Fungus will restore a small portion of [Stamina](Stamina%20bar.md).

Bouncing 40 meters off a Bounce Fungus grants the player the [![Mycoacrobatics Badge](../../images/Mycoacrobatics_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mycoacrobatics_Badge) [Mycoacrobatics Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mycoacrobatics_Badge).

## Trivia

- Albeit difficult, the Bounce Fungus can be thrown sideways onto a wall to be used as a ramshackle [![](../../images/Piton.png)](Piton.md) [Piton](Piton.md) due to its stamina-restoring property. Note that climbing over the Bounce Fungus without proper timing between bounces may cause the player to be launched off.
- The bottom side stem of the Bounce Fungus is not bouncy and can be used as a small platform.
