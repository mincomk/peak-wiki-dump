The **Energy Drink** is a packaged [food](Food.md) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* found in regular or large [luggage](Luggage.md).

Drinking an Energy Drink will immediately remove [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) **30** [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) and [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **50** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy), and the player will gain 11 seconds of both 50% bonus run speed and 150% bonus climb speed, with the maximum duration being shortened to 8 seconds after you begin climbing. While these effects are active, you cannot gain any [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy). After the effect ends, the player immediately receives [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) **25** [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy).

[Cooking](Cooking.md) an Energy Drink will cause it to explode upon consumption, causing no [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) but will knock-back players within **9.6 meters** of the explosion, including the user. Additionally, the duration of the effect is increased to 15 seconds, shortening to 12 seconds after you begin climbing, and no additional [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) is gained afterwards, although previously removed [![Drowsy](../../images/Status_Drowsy.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) [Drowsy](Stamina%20bar.md#Drowsy) is still returned.
