# Day-Night Cycle

The **Day-Night Cycle** in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)* lasts **10 minutes** of real time. The day begins at 05:00 (5 AM, game time) and ends at 21:00 (9 PM). One hour in-game is equivalent to **25 seconds** of real time, which gives the duration of a day of 400 seconds or ~6.67 minutes. The expedition starts at 09:00 (9 AM).

The Day-Night Cycle controls the direction and intensity of the sun. It also controls whether the [Mesa](Mesa.md)'s hot [sun](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Sun) affects the players and whether the night on [Ascent 5](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ascent_5) and higher will inflict [![Cold](../../images/Status_Cold.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) [Cold](Stamina%20bar.md#Cold) (~100 total).
