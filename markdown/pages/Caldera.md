# Caldera

The **Caldera** is one of two possible fourth [biomes](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Biomes) (along with [Gloom](Gloom.md)) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*, following the [Alpine](Alpine.md) or [Mesa](Mesa.md) and preceding [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md). It is a large lake of [lava](Lava.md) with various tall rock formations.

The main hazard of the Caldera is its [lava](#Lava), which rises and falls periodically. Condors also dwell within the Caldera, which scouts must be vigilant of.

At the end of the Caldera is a [temple](#Temple) with an [Ancient Statue](Ancient%20Statue.md). The temple leads into a tunnel in the side of a volcano. Lighting the [campfire](Campfire.md) inside the tunnel dispels the [fog](Fog.md) wall blocking access to [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md) and grants all living [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) the [![Volcanology Badge](../../images/Volcanology_Badge.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Volcanology_Badge) [Volcanology Badge](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Volcanology_Badge).

The Caldera does not have any additional time pressure mechanics, such as the [fog](Fog.md) or [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md)'s rising lava. Cold nights from [Ascent 5](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ascent_5) and later have no effect in the Caldera.

## Areas

### Rock Towers

---

[![Caldera Location.png](../../images/Caldera_Location.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ACaldera_Location.png)

The Caldera primarily consists of big tall **rock towers**. [Luggage](Luggage.md) and [bird nests](Big%20Egg.md) can sometimes be found on these rocks. Even though the rocks are tall, they usually feature many horizontal surfaces where [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) can rest. Some of these towers may be **hot rock towers**.

Chains connecting two neighboring rock towers can often be found. They function identically to the [vines](Vines.md) in the [Tropics](Tropics.md).

Occasionally, the rocks will [start to glow and burn](#Flare-ups), inflicting [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) on all [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) standing too close or caught directly in the fire.

### Temple

---

[![Caldera temple.png](../../images/Caldera_temple.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/File%3ACaldera_temple.png)

On the opposite end of the Caldera is a large **temple**. [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) will need to climb up multiple vertical walls to reach the top of the temple. The top of the temple houses an [Ancient Statue](Ancient%20Statue.md) and multiple [luggage](Luggage.md) boxes.

Behind the statue is a large bridge that leads into a tunnel within the side of the volcano, leading to the [campfire](Campfire.md) before [The Kiln](The%20Kiln.md).

## Tips & Tricks

- The Caldera is often very tricky (or outright impossible) to pass without walking on the burning hot ground uncovered whenever the lava level falls. Plan your route when the lava is high, and begin moving as soon as it falls again. You will take some [![Heat](../../images/Status_Heat.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) [Heat](Stamina%20bar.md#Heat) damage, but you should be fine as long as you have enough [stamina](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Stamina) to climb back up again, away from the rising lava. [![](../../images/Energy_Drink.png)](Energy%20Drink.md) [Energy Drinks](Energy%20Drink.md) and [![](../../images/Sports_Drink.png)](Sports%20Drink.md) [Sports Drinks](Sports%20Drink.md) can also help mitigate the heat.
- Be careful with dropped [equipment](Equipment.md) near the lava, as it may [cook](Cooking.md) it, destroying it in the process.
- Whilst [Condors](Condor.md) don't directly harm players, the drop into the nest may cause [![Injury](../../images/Status_Injury.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury) [Injury](Stamina%20bar.md#Injury), and the dangerous locations of the nests atop hot rock towers could provide a challenge if players are kidnapped, so it is best to try to avoid them. However, the nests will usually contain [![](../../images/Egg.png)](Egg.md) [Eggs](Egg.md) so if players are desperately hungry and can't locate any nearby nests or [luggage](Luggage.md), having a Condor kidnap a player may prove tactical.
- A [![](../../images/The_Book_of_Bones.png)](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_Bones) [Book of Bones](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_Bones) will make most of the hazards in this area trivial, as you can walk through hot areas with no consequence as long as you avoid contact with the lava itself. A [![](../../images/Glider.png)](Glider.md) [Glider](Glider.md) will allow players to glide above much of the lava floor between rocks.

## Trivia

- In geology, a [caldera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caldera) is "a large, cauldron-like hollow that forms shortly after the emptying of a magma chamber in a volcanic eruption."
- When you are in this biome, your Steam Now Playing will say "Crossing the Caldera".
