The **Anti-Rope Spool** is a [deployable](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Deployable) [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item) in *[PEAK](Peak%20%28game%29.md)*. It provides a total of **12.5 meters** climbable anti-gravity rope, placeable in custom lengths measuring from **0.75m** to **10m** long. The rope will float upwards, allowing [Scouts](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Scouts) to access hard to reach places.

Being a [mystical item](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Mystical_item), the Anti-Rope Spool can only be found in [Ancient Statues](Ancient%20Statue.md) and inside [Ancient Luggage](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_Luggage).

The Anti-Rope Spool has a negative weight and will reduce the total weight carried by the [Scout](Scout.md) (note that [Ascent 3's](Ascents.md) weight modification still applies to the Anti-Rope Spool). Dropping an Anti-Rope Spool will cause it to float up and away.

Cooking an Anti-Rope Spool immediately incinerates it, making it unusable (it retains its negative [![Weight](../../images/Status_Weight.png)](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight) [Weight](Stamina%20bar.md#Weight), however).

## Usage

When placing rope, first have the spool in hand and use the scroll wheel to unravel the rope to the desired length. To secure the rope, hold ![Mouse left.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Mouse_left.png/25px-Mouse_left.png?60ed49) for 2 seconds while aiming at the surface where the knot should be tied. A preview of the rope's anchor will shown in blue when the surface is valid, or red when the attempting to tie to an invalid surface.

Rope can be grabbed by using the interact key (![Keyboard e.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_e.png/25px-Keyboard_e.png?5e3847)) on it, and climbing is done using forward and backward movement (![Keyboard w.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_w.png/25px-Keyboard_w.png?23471e) / ![Keyboard s.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_s.png/25px-Keyboard_s.png?c6f457)). To let go of the rope, simply jump (![Keyboard space.png](https://commons.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Keyboard_space.png/25px-Keyboard_space.png?9ad9c4)).
